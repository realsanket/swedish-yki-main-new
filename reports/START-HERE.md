# Start here: how to study from these documents (no website needed)

*16 November 2026. Everything you need is in this folder. Open the files in any Markdown viewer (VS Code preview, Obsidian, a browser extension) or paste a file into a chat. All Swedish in these files is AI-written and has **not** been checked by a teacher: ask your teacher to check the core sheet first.*

## 2. First read, in this order (about 40 minutes, once)

1. `YKI-CORE-SHEET.md`: "The three layers" and "How to use this sheet" (the first page). Then skim sections 1-4 so you know what the answer shapes and the openings look like.
2. `YKI-EXAM-FIRST-PLAN.md`: section 1 (summary and the daily load rules) and section 3.1 (the daily lesson format). Skip the rest for now.
3. `YKI-LEARNER-DAYS-1-14.md`: the top of the file (how to read Green/Amber/Red and the openings table), then Day 1.

## 3. What a study day looks like

A normal day is **60 minutes** (a light day is about 35). The plan's blocks: review 0-8, new language 8-18, speaking 18-33, writing 33-48, listening or reading 48-56, error log 56-60. On a 90-minute day, stretch each block a little.

**Days 1-14:** open today's page in `YKI-LEARNER-DAYS-1-14.md` and do its steps one at a time. Check your answers in the "back of the file" section.

**Days 15-84:** open `YKI-EXAM-FIRST-CURRICULUM.md` and search for `### Day 23` (or your day). Read only that entry. Each entry has the same parts:

| Part | What it means |
|---|---|
| **What we learn today** | The Swedish lines of the day, with English. |
| **New patterns** | How many new sentence shapes the day holds (at most 6) and how many cards (at most 8). |
| **Hear it first** | Printed on Days 1-14 only. From Day 15 the rule still applies (load rule 4 in the core sheet): hear every new line from your teacher, a Swedish voice or your book's audio *before* you say it. |
| **Why it matters** | The exam task this day trains. |
| **How we learn it** | Minute-by-minute steps. |
| **Speaking / Writing output** | The exact task to record and to type. |
| **Done when** | Green, Amber or Red: see below. |
| **Typical errors** | Wrong form, then right form: check yourself against them. |
| **Book practice** | One or two tasks from your practice book; they replace a prompt of the day, they are not extra. |

If you want to see the whole calendar at a glance, use the table in section 5 of the plan (one row per day, with the cards that come back for review in the last column).

## 4. How to mark a day: Green, Amber, Red

- **Green:** you did the task on your own and covered every point. Go on to the next day.
- **Amber:** you got there, but slowly, or one mistake kept coming back. Write it in your error list and go on. Do not repeat the day.
- **Red:** you misunderstood the card, could not answer, or needed to read the model. Do the day again tomorrow with new details.
- A forgotten point (for example you left out your phone number) is Amber. A spelling slip or a wrong tense that the listener would still understand is not Red.

## 5. Keeping track without the website

- **Cards:** at most 8 new cards a day, and review stops after 8 minutes. Use paper cards or any flashcard app. Each day's review list is in the calendar table (plan section 5) and the schedule is in plan section 3.3 (1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days later).
- **Error list:** at most 3 error cards a day; how to write them is in plan section 3.6.
- **Pilot log:** Days 1-7 only. Fill in one row a day in `YKI-PILOT-LOG.md`. After Day 7 read its "How to read the result" part: it tells you whether 60 minutes is realistic for you.
- **Mocks:** Days 28, 56 and 84 are mock days (plan section 6). Your teacher or a helper should give you prompts you have not seen. Do not use the book tasks from `teacher-only/`.

## 6. Audio and a teacher

- You need to **hear** the Swedish, not only read it. Ask your teacher to record the lines in `YKI-AUDIO-LIST-DAYS-1-14.md`, or use a Swedish text-to-speech voice for the lines marked "Voice". Your practice book's own audio (QR codes) covers the book's tasks, not these lines.
- Ask your teacher to check the core sheet and Days 1-14 first. Their corrections win over anything in these files.

## 7. Words you will see

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **G1-G10** | The ten grammar points (plan section 2.9). |
| **T1-T20** | Answer templates (plan 2.3; T17-T20 are in their own file). They are a reference library, not a list to memorise. |
| **Engines** | The six answer shapes in the core sheet: Ask, Fix, Social, Opinion, Describe, Story. Pick one main engine per task and borrow moves from the others. |
| **Frame** | A sentence with a gap that you fill, for example *Jag bor i [___].* |
| **Pattern** | One independent sentence shape you can recall (the unit used to count new material). |
| **Seen practice** | Any prompt printed in these files. Mocks that count use prompts you have not seen. |

## 8. What is not decided, and what is not tested

- The exam date and which two subtests you take are chosen only when you book. Nothing here asks you to decide now.
- The minute counts and word counts are this course's own targets, not exam rules, and nobody has timed a real learner yet.
- The first seven days are a pilot: if a day is too much, say so in the pilot log and the day will be split.
