## How to use this sheet

- **Learn frames, not whole answers.** A frame is a sentence with a gap [ ] that you fill with the card's details.
- **Hear every line before you say it:** from your teacher, a Swedish voice, or your practice book's audio.
- **Small doses:** at most 6 new frames and 8 new cards a day. Review stops after 8 minutes.
- **On a task card:** underline what it asks for, pick the answer shape (the "engine"), open with the phone or letter frame, fill the shape, and stop when every point is covered.
- **With an AI chat:** paste this sheet in and ask it to quiz you from the English. It is not an official rater, and its Swedish can be wrong.

## 1. Five grammar rules these frames use

1. **The verb comes second.** If a time, a place or a word like *tyvärr* (unfortunately) comes first, the subject moves after the verb: *I dag jobbar jag hemma.* (Today I'm working at home.) Never *I dag jag jobbar*.
2. **Questions.** After a question word, the verb comes straight away: *Var bor du?* (Where do you live?) A yes/no question starts with the verb: *Har du tid i morgon?* (Do you have time tomorrow?)
3. ***inte* (not)** comes after the verb in a main sentence (*Jag kan inte komma.* I can't come), but **before** the verb after *att, eftersom, om* and *när*: *Problemet är att den inte startar.* (The problem is that it doesn't start.) / *… eftersom jag inte har tid.* (… because I don't have time.) *redan* (already) moves the same way.
4. **One verb form for every person:** *jag bor, du bor, vi bor.* Never put *är* in front of another verb: *Jag bor i Esbo*, not *Jag är bor i Esbo*. After *kan, vill, måste, ska, skulle* and *brukar* comes the base form, with no *att*: *Jag vill boka en tid.* (I want to book an appointment.)
5. ***den, det, de* (it, it, they).** *den* for an *en*-word (*en cykel*: *Den är tre år gammal.*); *det* for an *ett*-word, with *-t* on the adjective (*ett skrivbord*: *Det är två år gammalt.*); *de* for a plural, with *-a* (*De är tre år gamla.*).

## 2. The six answer shapes ("engines")

Pick one engine per task: it tells you what to say, in what order.

### Engine 1: Ask (request, booking, information)

**Shape:** who I am → what I want → one or two questions → confirm → thanks.

**Example, a call to book a dentist (the parts in "…" are the other voice):**
*God morgon! Det här är Alex Kumar. Jag ringer för att boka en tid.* … *Tyvärr inte, då jobbar jag. Har ni en tid efter klockan fyra?* … *Det passar bra. Hur mycket kostar besöket?* … *Tack för hjälpen! Hej då!*
(Good morning! This is Alex Kumar. I'm calling to book an appointment. … Unfortunately not, I'm working then. Do you have a time after four? … That suits me. How much does the visit cost? … Thanks for the help! Bye!)

### Engine 2: Fix (complaint, repair, asking someone to stop)

**Shape:** what is wrong → since when → why it matters → what I want → close.

**Example, a call to a repair firm (an official sample type):**
*Hej, det här är Alex Kumar. Jag ringer angående min tvättmaskin. Den är fortfarande trasig. En reparatör var här i tisdags, men den fungerar fortfarande inte. Vi är fyra personer och vi behöver tvättmaskinen varje dag. Kan ni komma i morgon eller på torsdag?*
(Hi, this is Alex Kumar. I'm calling about my washing machine. It is still broken. A repair person was here last Tuesday, but it still doesn't work. We are four people and we need the washing machine every day. Can you come tomorrow or on Thursday?)

### Engine 3: Social (react, thank, apologise, invite, refuse)

**Shape:** react or say the news → one detail or reason → a question, an offer or a new time → close.

**Example, a 20-second voicemail saying you will be late (an official sample type):**
*Hej Sara, det är Alex. Förlåt, jag blir ungefär en kvart försenad, eftersom bussen inte har kommit. Jag är där kvart över sex. Kan du vänta på mig vid ingången? Vi ses!*
(Hi Sara, it's Alex. Sorry, I'll be about a quarter of an hour late, because the bus hasn't come. I'll be there at quarter past six. Can you wait for me at the entrance? See you!)

### Engine 4: Opinion (your view, and advice)

**Shape:** view → reason + example → reason → the other side → conclusion. For advice: *Jag tycker att du ska …* + a reason + an example.

**Example, a spoken opinion (*Det är bättre att bo i en stad än på landet. Håller du med?*):**
*Jag tycker att det är bättre att bo i en stad. För det första är det lättare att leva utan bil. Till exempel går bussar och tåg ofta. För det andra finns det många jobb i staden. Å andra sidan är det dyrt att bo i en stad. Därför tycker jag att det är bättre att bo i en stad.*
(I think it is better to live in a city. First, it is easier to live without a car. For example, buses and trains run often. Second, there are many jobs in the city. On the other hand, it is expensive to live in a city. That is why I think it is better to live in a city.)

In speaking, two reasons are enough. A letter to the editor (an official sample type) opens with the topic (*Jag vill skriva om …*) and needs at least three reasons (*För det tredje …*).

### Engine 5: Describe (a person, a habit, a place, something you sell)

**Shape:** what or who → two details → what I usually do → why it matters. As a seller: age, condition, price, a time to come, your address.

**Example, a talk about a person important to you (an official sample type):**
*Jag vill berätta om min vän Ravi. Vi har känt varandra i femton år. Ravi är lång och har kort svart hår. Vi brukar prata i telefon en gång i veckan. Han är viktig för mig eftersom han alltid finns där när jag behöver honom.*
(I want to tell you about my friend Ravi. We have known each other for fifteen years. Ravi is tall and has short black hair. We usually talk on the phone once a week. He is important to me because he is always there when I need him.)

### Engine 6: Story (what happened, and reports)

**Shape:** when and where → first, then, in the end → how it felt → now. For a report to an office or the police, add a description and your contact details.

**Example, a message explaining why you were late:**
*Hej Sara! Förlåt att jag kom så sent i går. Först kom bussen för sent, och sedan missade jag tåget. Till slut tog jag en taxi, men det var mycket trafik. Det var jobbigt, eftersom jag ville vara med från början. Kram, Alex*
(Hi Sara! Sorry I came so late yesterday. First the bus came too late, and then I missed the train. In the end I took a taxi, but there was a lot of traffic. It was hard, because I wanted to be there from the start. Hugs, Alex)

## 3. The two frames: how to open and close

**Phone frame (every call).**
- You call: *Hej, det här är [namn].* (Hi, this is …) *Jag ringer angående [___].* (I'm calling about …)
- Confirm a time: *Okej, då säger vi [torsdag klockan tio].* (OK, then we'll say Thursday at ten.)
- Close: *Tack för hjälpen. Hej då!* (Thanks for the help. Bye!)

**Letter frame.**
- To a friend: *Hej [namn]!* (Hi …!) … *Hälsningar, [namn]* (Regards, …)
- To a firm or an office: *Hej,* (or *Bästa mottagare,* Dear recipient) … *Jag skriver om [___].* (I am writing about …) … *Ni når mig på [telefonnummer] eller [e-postadress].* (You can reach me on … or …) … *Med vänliga hälsningar, [för- och efternamn]* (Kind regards, …)

## 4. The seven official sample tasks: what each must contain

The public practice tasks for this test. A missing point counts as an error, so tick each one.

1. **Talk about a person important to you** (speaking: 1 minute to prepare, 1.5 minutes to talk). The sample names only the topic. The plan's six points: who; how long you have known them; looks and personality; what you do together; why they matter; a memory or a wish.
2. **A buyer phones about your "for sale" ad** (speaking: turns of about 10–25 seconds). Answer the questions (still for sale? age? condition? price? a lower offer?), suggest a time and say where you live.
3. **You phone a repair firm about something still broken** (speaking: 6 turns). Who you are; what is broken; that it is still broken after a repair visit, and when; a protest with a reason; what you want (a new time, your address).
4. **A voicemail saying you will be late** (speaking: 20 seconds). Who you are; that you will be late; a short reason; when you will be there.
5. **An e-mail to a friend about an event** (writing). What; when; where; two more details; a proper opening and closing. The plan adds an invitation question (*Vill du följa med?*).
6. **A letter to the editor** (writing). The topic; your opinion; at least three reasons. The plan adds an example, the other side and a conclusion that matches your opinion.
7. **A complaint about a bill** (writing, formal). Which bill (number, date and amount); at least two reasons why it is too high; an amount you think is fair; your contact details; a formal opening and closing.

## 5. The core frames (about 60)

**Me (6)**
- *Jag heter [___].* (My name is …)
- *Jag kommer från [___].* (I come from …)
- *Jag bor i [___].* (I live in …)
- *Jag talar [___].* (I speak …)
- *Jag lär mig svenska.* (I am learning Swedish.)
- *Jag jobbar som [___].* (I work as …)

**Phone frame (4) and letter frame (5):** the lines in section 3.

**Ask (6)**
- *Jag skulle vilja [boka en tid].* (I would like to book an appointment.)
- *Har ni en ledig tid [på tisdag]?* (Do you have a free time on Tuesday?)
- *Passar det [på torsdag klockan tio]?* (Does Thursday at ten suit you?)
- *Hur mycket kostar [besöket]?* (How much does the visit cost?)
- *Jag undrar om [det finns lediga platser].* (I wonder if there are free places.)
- *När kan jag [komma]?* (When can I come?)

**Fix (8)**
- *[Den] är fortfarande trasig.* (It is still broken.)
- *Problemet är att [den inte startar].* (The problem is that it doesn't start.)
- *En reparatör var här [i tisdags], men det hjälpte inte.* (A repair person was here last Tuesday, but it didn't help.)
- *Kan ni [komma i morgon eller på torsdag]?* (Can you come tomorrow or on Thursday?)
- *Jag vill [få pengarna tillbaka].* (I want to get my money back.)
- *Fakturan är för hög av två skäl.* (The bill is too high, for two reasons.)
- *Jag tycker att [___] euro är ett rimligt belopp.* (I think … euros is a reasonable amount.)
- *Ursäkta, skulle du kunna [prata lite tystare]?* (Excuse me, could you talk a bit more quietly?)

**Social (7)**
- *Tack för [inbjudan].* (Thanks for the invitation.)
- *Tyvärr kan jag inte [komma], eftersom [jag jobbar].* (Unfortunately I can't come, because I'm working.)
- *Kan vi ses [på lördag] i stället?* (Can we meet on Saturday instead?)
- *Förlåt, jag blir [en kvart] försenad, eftersom [___].* (Sorry, I'll be a quarter of an hour late, because …)
- *Jag är där [kvart över sex].* (I'll be there at quarter past six.)
- *Det är [på lördag] klockan [___] [i/på ___].* (It is on Saturday at … in/at …)
- *Vill du [följa med]?* (Do you want to come along?)

**Opinion (7)**
- *Jag tycker att [___].* (I think that …)
- *Jag håller med.* / *Jag håller inte med.* (I agree. / I don't agree.)
- *För det första [___]. För det andra [___]. För det tredje [___].* (First … Second … Third … The verb comes next: *För det första sparar man tid.*)
- *Till exempel [___].* (For example …)
- *Å andra sidan [___].* (On the other hand …)
- *Därför tycker jag att [___].* (That is why I think that …)
- *Jag tycker att du ska [___]. Du kan till exempel [___].* (I think you should … You can for example … For advice.)

**Describe (11)**
- A person:
  - *Jag vill berätta om [___].* (I want to tell you about …)
  - *Hon är [snäll och glad].* (She is kind and cheerful.)
  - *Jag har känt henne [i tio år].* (I have known her for ten years.)
  - *Vi brukar [laga mat tillsammans].* (We usually cook together.)
  - *Hon är viktig för mig eftersom [___].* (She is important to me because …)
- A habit, a place or a thing:
  - *Jag brukar [simma två gånger i veckan].* (I usually swim twice a week.)
  - *Det finns [en bastu och en brygga].* (There is a sauna and a jetty.)
  - *Den ligger [nära centrum].* (It is near the centre. With an *ett*-word: *Det ligger …*)
- Something you sell:
  - *Ja, den finns kvar. Den är [tre] år gammal och i bra skick.* (Yes, it's still for sale. It is three years old and in good condition.)
  - *Den kostar [150] euro, men du kan få den för [130] euro.* (It costs 150 euros, but you can have it for 130.)
  - *Du kan komma [i morgon efter klockan sex]. Jag bor på [Solvägen 5] i [Esbo].* (You can come tomorrow after six. I live at Solvägen 5 in Espoo.)

**Story (5)**
- *[Förra veckan] hände något [konstigt].* (Last week something strange happened.)
- *Först [___]. Sedan [___]. Till slut [___].* (First … Then … In the end … The verb comes next after each.)
- *Det var [jobbigt], eftersom [___].* (It was hard, because …)
- *Jag har tappat bort [min plånbok].* (I have lost my wallet.)
- *Det hände [i går kväll] [på bussen].* (It happened last night on the bus.)

For a report, borrow *Kan ni [kontakta mig]?* (Can you contact me?) from Fix.

## 6. Twelve reason pairs

Each pair has a "for" and an "against" side, so you can argue either way. Follow a reason with *Till exempel …* and a detail from the task. Use only reasons that fit.

| Topic | For (the positive side) | Against (the negative side) |
|---|---|---|
| Time | *Det sparar tid.* (It saves time.) | *Det tar mycket tid.* (It takes a lot of time.) |
| Cost | *Det är billigare.* (It is cheaper.) | *Det är dyrt.* (It is expensive.) |
| Health | *Det är bra för hälsan.* (It is good for your health.) | *Man rör sig för lite.* (You move too little.) |
| Environment | *Det är bra för miljön.* (It is good for the environment.) | *Det är dåligt för miljön.* (It is bad for the environment.) |
| Stress | *Man blir mindre stressad.* (You get less stressed.) | *Man blir lätt stressad.* (You easily get stressed.) |
| People | *Man träffar nya människor.* (You meet new people.) | *Man kan känna sig ensam.* (You can feel lonely.) |
| Safety | *Det är tryggt.* (It feels safe.) | *Det kan vara farligt.* (It can be dangerous.) |
| Choice | *Man kan välja själv.* (You can choose yourself.) | *Det passar inte alla.* (It does not suit everyone.) |
| Ease | *Det gör vardagen enklare.* (It makes everyday life easier.) | *Det kan vara svårt för äldre människor.* (It can be hard for older people.) |
| Children | *Det är bra för barnen.* (It is good for the children.) | *Barnen sitter för mycket framför skärmen.* (Children sit too much in front of the screen.) |
| Quality | *Kvaliteten är ofta bättre.* (The quality is often better.) | *Servicen blir sämre.* (The service gets worse.) |
| Flexibility | *Man kan göra det när som helst.* (You can do it at any time.) | *Det kan bli fel.* (Things can go wrong.) |

*man* means "you" or "people in general". After *En nackdel är att* (a disadvantage is that), *inte* moves before the verb: *En nackdel är att det inte passar alla.*

## 7. Ten reactions

Shape: react → one detail → a question, an offer or a close. [bank N] is the number in the plan's list of 30. Example: *Grattis! Vad roligt att du har fått jobbet! När börjar du?* (Congratulations! How nice that you got the job! When do you start?)

1. *Grattis! Vad roligt!* (Congratulations! How nice!) [bank 1]
2. *Vad tråkigt att höra.* (I'm sorry to hear that.) [bank 6]
3. *Krya på dig!* (Get well soon! Only for illness.) [bank 8]
4. *Jag beklagar sorgen.* (I'm sorry for your loss. Only for a death.) [bank 12]
5. *Tack så mycket för hjälpen!* (Thank you very much for the help!) [bank 14]
6. *Det gör inget.* (It doesn't matter. The reply to an apology.) [bank 19]
7. *Förlåt att jag är sen.* (Sorry I'm late. When you are already late.) [bank 22]
8. *Förlåt, jag blir lite sen.* (Sorry, I'll be a bit late. Before the meeting time.) [bank 23]
9. *Ja, gärna!* (Yes, I'd love to! A plain yes.) [bank 25]
10. *Tyvärr kan jag inte.* (Unfortunately I can't.) [bank 28]

## 8. Eight rescue phrases

Use them instead of going silent. Never switch to English.

1. *Förlåt, jag menar …* (Sorry, I mean … Correct yourself once, then carry on.)
2. *Jag säger det på ett annat sätt.* (I'll say it another way.)
3. *Jag kommer inte på ordet, men det är något man använder för att …* (I can't think of the word, but it's something you use to …)
4. *Det är som en …* (It's like a …)
5. *Jag är inte säker, men jag tror att …* (I'm not sure, but I think that …)
6. *Det beror på …* (It depends on …)
7. *Till exempel …* (For example …)
8. *Det var allt jag ville säga.* (That was all I wanted to say.)

If you miss what the recorded voice said, you cannot ask it to repeat. Say *Okej.* and give the next point from your card.

You need to understand these, not to say them.

**Layer 1: the present of all 30** (base form → present).

| *gå → går* | go, walk | *skicka → skickar* | send |
| *komma → kommer* | come | *boka → bokar* | book |

| *ta → tar* | take | *träffa → träffar* | meet |

| *bli → blir* | become, get | *tycka → tycker* | think (an opinion) |

**Layer 2: the past of 12.** *var* (was), *köpte* (bought), *ringde* (phoned), *kom* (came), *glömde* (forgot), *gick* (went), *hjälpte* (helped), *tog* (took), *blev* (became, got), *fick* (got), *sa* (said), *bodde* (lived). These are the past forms the plan's own Swedish uses most. Use the past with a finished time: *Jag köpte den i måndags.* (I bought it last Monday.)

**Layer 3: the perfect, only inside chunks.** Learn these whole: *Jag har bott i Finland i fem år.* (I have lived in Finland for five years.) / *Jag har redan betalat.* (I have already paid.) / *Har ni fått mitt mejl?* (Have you got my e-mail?)

## 11. The nine daily load rules

1. At most 6 new frames and 8 new cards a day.
2. One grammar point a day, stated in one sentence.
3. Review stops at 8 minutes, finished or not.
4. Hear each new line before saying it.
5. One speaking task and one typed task. A book task replaces the day's own prompt.
6. A day is 60 minutes. If a block is added, another is removed.
7. A day is done when a changed version of the task works, not when a list is recited fast.
8. If the "done when" fails, repeat the day tomorrow with new details.
9. If a day holds more than rule 1 allows, split it into day a and day b. Day b uses the same tasks with new details. The calendar slips, and that is intended.

## 12. The first two days

### Day 1: say who you are

**New frames (5):** *Jag heter [Alex].* (My name is …) / *Jag kommer från [Indien].* (I come from …) / *Jag bor i [Esbo].* (I live in …) / *Jag talar [engelska, hindi och marathi].* (I speak …) / *Jag lär mig svenska.* (I am learning Swedish.)

**Questions to understand (4):** *Vad heter du?* (What is your name?) / *Var kommer du ifrån?* (Where are you from?) / *Var bor du?* (Where do you live?) / *Vilka språk talar du?* (Which languages do you speak?)

**Instruction words to recognise (2):** *berätta* (tell), *skriv* (write).

**The one grammar fact:** the verb has the same form for every person: *jag bor, du bor*.

| Minutes | What you do |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | **Listen.** Hear each frame three times, English in view. Repeat it. |
| 10–20 | **Recall and swap.** Say each frame from the English, then with three new details. |
| 20–30 | **Questions.** Hear the four questions, mixed. Answer each in a full sentence within 10 seconds. |
| 30–40 | **Speaking.** Card: *Berätta om dig själv.* (Tell about yourself.) Record five sentences, listen once, redo once. |
| 40–50 | **Typing.** Set up å, ä and ö. Type the five sentences from memory, compare with the model, fix, type again. |
| 50–55 | **Change check.** Answer the questions as Sara: from Sweden, lives in Åbo, speaks Swedish and Finnish. |
| 55–60 | **Cards:** 5 frames and 3 questions (8 cards). Start an error log. |

**Model:** *Hej! Jag heter Alex. Jag kommer från Indien. Jag bor i Esbo. Jag talar engelska, hindi och marathi. Jag lär mig svenska.*

**Done when:** you answer 4 of 4 questions without reading, and the typed text has no more than 2 spelling slips. If not, repeat Day 1 tomorrow with new details.

### Day 2: say more, and ask back

**New frames (5):** *Jag jobbar som [ingenjör].* (I work as …) / *Jag har bott i Finland i [fem] år.* (I have lived in Finland for … years. Learn it as one piece.) / *Vad heter du?* (now you ask it) / *Var bor du?* (now you ask it) / *Och du?* (And you?)

**Questions to understand (2 more):** *Vad jobbar du med?* (What do you do for work?) / *Hur länge har du bott i Finland?* (How long have you lived in Finland?)

**Numbers 0–12:** *noll, ett, två, tre, fyra, fem, sex, sju, åtta, nio, tio, elva, tolv.*

**Instruction words to recognise (2):** *fråga* (ask), *svara* (answer).

**The one grammar fact:** in a question, the verb comes straight after the question word: *Var bor du?*, not *Var du bor?*

| Minutes | What you do |
|---|---|
| 0–8 | **Review** the 8 cards from Day 1, aloud. |
| 8–18 | **Listen and repeat** the frames and numbers 0–12. |
| 18–28 | **Answers into questions:** *Jag bor i Esbo.* → *Var bor du?* Then answer all six questions, mixed. |
| 28–40 | **Speaking.** A helper asks the four Day 1–2 questions about name, home, work and years in Finland. Answer each in one sentence; twice, add *Och du?* Record, listen once, redo once. |
| 40–50 | **Typing.** Card: *Skriv till en ny kurskamrat. Berätta om dig själv och ställ två frågor.* (Write to a new course-mate. Tell about yourself and ask two questions.) |
| 50–55 | **Numbers.** Hear 8 numbers from 0 to 12; write the digits. |
| 55–60 | **Cards (8 at most):** 2 frames, 3 question cards, and the numbers you missed. |

**Model:** *Hej! Jag heter Alex och jag kommer från Indien. Jag bor i Esbo. Jag har bott i Finland i fem år. Jag jobbar som ingenjör. Jag lär mig svenska. Vad heter du? Var bor du? Hälsningar, Alex*

**Done when:** you answer 6 of 6 questions in full sentences, both of your own questions have the verb before *du*, and the message has a greeting, five facts, two questions and a closing.
