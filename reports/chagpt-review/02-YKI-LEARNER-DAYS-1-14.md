# Swedish YKI: Days 1–14, one page a day

[Unreviewed Swedish: have a teacher check it]

Rewritten on 16 November 2026 (third review). The reference layer behind these pages is the curriculum file (`YKI-EXAM-FIRST-CURRICULUM.md`, Days 1–14).

**How to use a page**
- **Audio first.** Every Swedish line in this file is in `YKI-AUDIO-LIST-DAYS-1-14.md`. Your teacher or a Swedish text-to-speech voice records it. Hear each line three times, then repeat it. (The book's QR audio has only the book's own tasks, not these lines.)
- **Bold** inside a Swedish line is the part you change. ~~Struck-through~~ Swedish is a mistake to avoid; never copy it.
- The words in brackets after a line say what each new word means.
- Models, keys and the helper's lines are at the back of this file. Look at a model only after your first try.

**How much is new each day (the counting rule)**
- A **pattern** is a sentence shape you must say or write from memory: a new word order, a new verb form, or a new opening or closing. A line with two sentences counts as two patterns.
- A simple swap is not new: another word in the same slot (another town, name, day, first word or helper verb), given as a swap word on the page. Nor is a pattern you already know.
- **Listening cards** (a question to understand, a task word) are cards, but not patterns.
- At most **6 new patterns** and **8 cards** a day. Each page lists its cards. A number you missed or an error card takes the place of the last card on the list; that card waits for the next day.

**How to read Green / Amber / Red**
- **Green:** you did the task on your own and gave every point. Spelling slips don't count. Go on.
- **Amber:** you got there, but slowly; or a mistake kept coming back; or one point was missing but the message still works; or you used the wrong tense but the listener still understands. Write the mistake in your error list and go on.
- **Red:** you misunderstood the card, could not answer, needed to read the model, or the message cannot be used (for example, nobody could write down your number). Do the day again tomorrow with new details.

**Openings and closings: choose them by the situation**

| Situation | Open with | Close with |
|---|---|---|
| Phone call | *Hej, det här är **Alex**.* | *Tack för hjälpen. Hej då!* |
| Voicemail to a friend | *Hej **Anna**, det är Alex.* | *Hej då!* |
| Message or e-mail to a friend | *Hej!* | *Hälsningar, Alex* |
| Formal e-mail | *Hej! Jag skriver angående **simhallen**.* | *Med vänliga hälsningar, Alex Kumar* |
| Your opinion (talk or text) | *Jag tycker att **det är en bra idé**.* | *Därför tycker jag att …* |
| A story or a description (talk) | *Jag vill berätta om **min helg**.* | |
| A request, face to face | *Ursäkta, kan du **hjälpa** mig?* | |
| Reacting to news | *Vad roligt!* / *Vad tråkigt att höra!* | |

Never open a hobby talk with a phone greeting, and never close it like a letter.

**Timers and length:** "Run it (40 s, then 30 s with a new day)" means: say it once with no timer; record it within the first time (a gentle limit); listen once for the points; then redo it with the change, within the second time. A good practice pace is about 80 words a minute: 20 seconds is about 25–33 words, 30 s about 40, 60 s about 80, 90 s about 120. Times are practice aims, not exam rules.

**Typing å, ä, ö:** a Finnish or Swedish keyboard has the keys (å is right of P; ö and ä are right of L). On a phone, hold down a or o. On a Mac with another layout: Option + a gives å; Option + u, then a, gives ä; Option + u, then o, gives ö. On Windows, add the Swedish or Finnish keyboard (Settings, then Time & language, then Language & region).

## Day 1: Say who you are

**Audio:** Day 1 in the audio list.

**New patterns (5)**
1. *Jag heter **Alex**.* My name is Alex. (*jag* I · *heter* am called)
2. *Jag kommer från **Indien**.* I come from India. (*kommer* come · *från* from) Swap: *Sverige* Sweden, *Nepal*.
3. *Jag bor i **Esbo**.* I live in Espoo. (*bor* live · *i* in) Swap: *Helsingfors* Helsinki, *Vanda* Vantaa, *Åbo* Turku.
4. *Jag talar **engelska, hindi och marathi**.* I speak English, Hindi and Marathi. (*talar* speak · *och* and) Swap: *svenska* Swedish, *finska* Finnish.
5. *Jag lär mig svenska.* I am learning Swedish. (*lär mig* learn: keep it as one piece)

**Understand** (listening only): *Vad heter du?* What's your name? (*vad* what · *du* you) · *Var kommer du ifrån?* Where are you from? (*var* where) · *Var bor du?* Where do you live? · *Vilka språk talar du?* Which languages do you speak? (*vilka* which · *språk* languages)

**Words:** *berätta* tell · *skriv* write
**Rule:** one verb form for every person: *jag bor, du bor.* Don't put *är* before another verb to make the present: *Jag bor*, not ~~Jag är bor~~.

**Steps** (no timer today)
1. Listen and repeat. (10 min)
2. Say each pattern from the English; then swap the bold part three times. (10 min)
3. Answer the four questions in full sentences. (10 min)
4. *Berätta om dig själv.* (Tell about yourself.) Record your five sentences; redo once. (10 min)
5. Set up å, ä, ö (top of file). Type the five sentences from memory; check with the model; fix. (10 min)
6. Answer the questions as Sara: *Sara*, from *Sverige*, lives in *Åbo*, speaks *svenska och finska*. (5 min)
7. Make the cards. Start your error list. (5 min)

**Cards (8):** 1–5 = patterns 1–5 (English on the front, Swedish on the back); 6–8 = listening cards (Swedish on the front, the meaning and your answer on the back): *Vad heter du?* · *Var kommer du ifrån?* · *Vilka språk talar du?*

**Done?**
- Green: you answer the four questions without reading; your recording and your typing give the five facts.
- Amber or Red: see the top. Watch for ~~Jag är bor~~.

## Day 2: Say more, and ask back

**Audio:** Day 2 in the audio list.

**New patterns (5)**
1. *Jag jobbar som **ingenjör**.* I work as an engineer. (*jobbar* work · *som* as; no *en* before a job) Swap: *lärare* teacher, *läkare* doctor, *programmerare* programmer.
2. *Jag har bott i Finland i **fem** år.* I have lived in Finland for five years. (*har bott* have lived · *i … år* for … years: keep it as one piece) Swap: any number from 1 to 12.
3. *Vad heter du?* Now you ask it: the question word, then the verb, then *du*.
4. *Och du?* And you?
5. *Hälsningar, Alex* Regards, Alex (closes a message to a friend; it opens with *Hej!*)
Same shape as pattern 3: *Var bor du?*

**Understand:** *Vad jobbar du med?* What do you do for work? · *Hur länge har du bott i Finland?* How long have you lived in Finland? (*hur länge* how long)

**What does the question want?** *Var?* a place · *Vad?* a thing or a name · *Hur länge?* a length of time (*i fem år*). Each later day adds its question word.

**Words:** *fråga* ask · *svara* answer · *ställ två frågor* ask two questions
**Numbers 0–12:** *noll, ett, två, tre, fyra, fem, sex, sju, åtta, nio, tio, elva, tolv*
**Rule:** after a question word, the verb comes next: *Var bor du?*, not ~~Var du bor?~~

**Steps**
1. Review your Day 1 cards. (8 min)
2. Listen and repeat, numbers too. (10 min)
3. Say what each question wants. (2 min)
4. Make the question from the answer: *Jag bor i Esbo.* → *Var bor du?* and *Jag heter Alex.* → *Vad heter du?* Then answer all six questions. (8 min)
5. A helper asks four questions (back of file); answer each, and twice add *Och du?* Record; redo. (12 min)
6. Type: *Skriv till en ny kurskamrat. Berätta om dig själv och ställ två frågor.* (Write to a new course-mate.) (10 min)
7. Write the 8 numbers your helper says. (5 min)
8. Cards. (5 min)

**Cards (8):** 1–5 = patterns 1–5; 6 = *Var bor du?*; 7 = listening card: the two Understand questions; 8 = word card: the three Words.

**Done?**
- Green: full answers on your own; the verb comes next in your two questions; the message has *Hej!*, your facts, two questions and *Hälsningar*.
- Amber or Red: see the top. Watch for ~~Var du bor?~~

## Day 3: two study days

Do Day 3a, then Day 3b on your next study day.

### Day 3a: Time first, verb second

**Audio:** Day 3a in the audio list.

**New patterns (4)**
1. *I dag jobbar jag hemma.* Today I'm working at home. (*i dag* today · *hemma* at home) Swap: *I morgon* tomorrow, *på kontoret* at the office.
2. *Nu bor jag i Esbo.* Now I live in Espoo. (*nu* now)
3. *På fritiden går jag på **bio**.* In my free time I go to the cinema. (*på fritiden* in my free time · *går på bio* go to the cinema) Swap: *konsert* a concert, *museum*.
4. *På morgonen tar jag **bussen**.* In the morning I take the bus. (*på morgonen* in the morning · *tar* take) Swap: *tåget* the train, *metron* the metro.

**Understand** (new ways to ask *Var bor du?*; your answer stays the same): *Var bor du nu?* · *I vilken stad bor du?* (*stad* town)
**Words:** *när* when (it wants a time) · *läs* read
**Numbers 13–20:** *tretton, fjorton, femton, sexton, sjutton, arton, nitton, tjugo*
**Rule:** the verb is the second unit of a statement. A time phrase (*i dag, på fritiden*) is one unit, so *jag* comes after the verb: *I dag jobbar jag*, not ~~I dag jag jobbar~~.

**Steps**
1. Review. (8 min)
2. Listen and repeat. Turn *Jag jobbar hemma i dag.* into pattern 1. Change the person: *Nu bor hon i Vanda.* (*hon* she · *han* he). Make the question from *Nu bor jag i Esbo.* (10 min)
3. *Berätta om dig själv*, with two time-first sentences. Run it (90 s, then 70 s with one change). (15 min)
4. Type to your teacher: *Hej Eva!* … *Hälsningar, Alex*, with two time-first sentences. (15 min)
5. Read every number on a notice aloud. (8 min)
6. Cards. (4 min)

**Cards (7):** 1–4 = patterns 1–4; 5 = listening card: the two Understand questions; 6 = word card: *när, läs*; 7 = the number from 13 to 20 you find hardest.

**Done?**
- Green: talk and message give your facts with two time-first sentences, verb before *jag*; one changed detail still works.
- Amber or Red: see the top. Red here: no time-first sentence without the model. Watch for ~~Nu jag bor~~.

### Day 3b: Where *inte* goes

**Audio:** Day 3b in the audio list.

**New patterns (6)**
1. *Jag kan inte komma **i dag**.* I can't come today. (*kan* can · *inte* not · *komma* come)
2. *I dag kan jag inte komma.* The same, time first.
3. *Jag har inte tid **i morgon**.* I don't have time tomorrow. (*har tid* have time) Swap: *på fredag*; time first: *I morgon har jag inte tid.*
4. *Kan vi ses på **torsdag** i stället?* Can we meet on Thursday instead? (*vi ses* we meet · *i stället* instead)
5. *Hej **Anna**, det är Alex.* Hi Anna, it's Alex. (a voicemail to a friend; *det är* it is)
6. *Hej då!* Bye!
Swap into the first place: *Tyvärr kan jag inte komma.* Unfortunately I can't come. (*tyvärr* unfortunately: it takes the first place, like a time word)

**Words:** *vem* who (*Vem kommer?* Who's coming?) · *lyssna* listen · *lämna ett meddelande* leave a message
**Days:** *måndag, tisdag, onsdag, torsdag, fredag, lördag, söndag* · *i dag, i morgon, i går* (today, tomorrow, yesterday)
**Rule:** *inte* comes right after the verb; with a time word first: the verb, *jag*, *inte*.

**Steps**
1. Review. (8 min)
2. Listen and repeat; say patterns 1 and 3 in both orders. (10 min)
3. Voicemail: *Du ska träffa din vän Anna i dag klockan fem, men du kan inte komma. Lämna ett meddelande och föreslå en annan dag.* (You're meeting Anna at five today but can't come; suggest another day.) Run it (40 s, then 30 s with a new friend and day). (15 min)
4. Text your colleague Mikko: no lunch today (*äta lunch* have lunch); suggest another day. (15 min)
5. On an opening-hours notice, find the days, the times and *stängt* (closed). (8 min)
6. Cards. (4 min)

**Cards (8):** 1–4 = patterns 1–4; 5 = patterns 5 and 6 (*Hej Anna, det är Alex.* … *Hej då!*); 6 = word card: *tyvärr, i stället, stängt*; 7 = word card: *vem, lyssna*; 8 = word card: *lämna ett meddelande*.

**Done?**
- Green: who you are, can't come, a short reason, a new day as a question, goodbye, on your own.
- Amber or Red: see the top. Watch for ~~Jag inte har tid~~.

## Day 4: Book an appointment

**Audio:** Day 4 in the audio list.

**New patterns (4)**
1. *Hej, det här är **Alex Kumar**.* Hi, this is Alex Kumar. (the phone opening; *det här* this)
2. *Jag skulle vilja **boka en tid**.* I would like to book an appointment. (*skulle vilja* would like · *boka* book · *en tid* a time, an appointment)
3. *Kan jag komma efter klockan **fyra**?* Can I come after four? (*efter* after · *klockan* o'clock)
4. *Tack för hjälpen.* Thanks for the help. (then *Hej då!*)
Known shapes, new words: *Jag kan inte komma på morgonen.* I can't come in the morning. · *Jag måste jobba på morgonen.* I have to work in the morning. (*måste* must) Swap: *på eftermiddagen* in the afternoon, *på kvällen* in the evening.

**Understand** (new ways to ask Day 1 questions): *Var någonstans bor du?* Where do you live? · *Varifrån kommer du?* Where are you from?
**Words:** *välj* choose · *be om* ask for
**Tens:** *tjugo, trettio, fyrtio, femtio, sextio, sjuttio, åttio, nittio, hundra* (20–100)
**Rule:** after *kan, vill, måste* and *skulle vilja* comes the base form, with no *att*: *Jag vill boka*, not ~~Jag vill att boka~~. In a yes/no question this verb comes first: *Kan jag komma?*

**Steps**
1. Review. (8 min)
2. Listen and repeat. Ladder: *Jag vill boka en tid.* → pattern 2 → *Jag kan komma efter klockan fyra.* → pattern 3. (10 min)
3. Call the health centre: *Ring hälsocentralen och boka en tid. Du kan inte komma på morgonen. Be om en tid efter klockan fyra.* Helper's lines: back of file. Run it (40 s a turn, then 30 s as a dentist call). (15 min)
4. Text the hairdresser (*frisören*): book a time, no mornings, ask for a time after four. (15 min)
5. Write the 8 numbers with tens your helper says. (8 min)
6. Cards. (4 min)

**Cards (8):** 1–4 = patterns 1–4; 5 = *Jag kan inte komma på morgonen.*; 6 = *Jag måste jobba på morgonen.*; 7 = listening card: the two Understand questions; 8 = word card: *välj, be om*.

**Done?**
- Green: who you are and what you want, no mornings, a later time as a question, thanks and goodbye; the dentist call works too.
- Amber or Red: see the top. Watch for ~~Jag vill att boka~~.

## Day 5: Say what you think

**Audio:** Day 5 in the audio list.

**New patterns (5)**
1. *Jag tycker att **det är en bra idé**.* I think it's a good idea. (*tycker att* think: an opinion · *bra* good)
2. *Jag tror att **hon kommer i morgon**.* I think she's coming tomorrow. (*tror att* think: a guess)
3. *Jag hoppas att **jag får jobbet**.* I hope I get the job. (*hoppas* hope · *får* get) Swap: *det blir bättre* it gets better.
4. *Problemet är att **den inte startar**.* The problem is that it doesn't start. Swap: *bussen inte kommer* the bus doesn't come.
5. *Jag är inte säker, men jag tror att …* I'm not sure, but I think that … (*säker* sure · *men* but)

**Understand** (new ways to ask about work): *Vad arbetar du med?* · *Vad har du för jobb?* Your answer stays *Jag jobbar som ingenjör.*
**Words:** *vilken / vilket / vilka* which · *rätt / fel* true / false
**Numbers:** *tjugoett* 21 · *trettiofem* 35 · *nittionio* 99. Easy to mix up: *tretton* 13 / *trettio* 30 · *fjorton* 14 / *fyrtio* 40 · *femton* 15 / *femtio* 50 · *sexton* 16 / *sextio* 60 · *sjutton* 17 / *sjuttio* 70 · *arton* 18 / *åttio* 80 · *nitton* 19 / *nittio* 90
**Rule:** after *att*, the person comes first, then *inte*, then the verb: *att den inte startar*, not ~~att den startar inte~~.

**Steps**
1. Review. (8 min)
2. Listen and repeat. Ladder: *Jag tycker att det är dyrt.* → *Jag tycker att bussen är dyr.* → *Jag tycker att bussen inte är dyr.* (*dyr* expensive) (10 min)
3. A friend asks: *Vad tycker du om bussarna där du bor?* (What do you think of the buses where you live?) Your opinion, one problem, one hope. Run it (40 s; then *Vad tycker du om vintern i Finland?*, 30 s). (15 min)
4. Type to a friend about your Swedish course: opinion, a problem with *inte*, a hope. (15 min)
5. Write the 8 numbers your helper says. (8 min)
6. Cards. (4 min)

**Cards (8):** 1–5 = patterns 1–5; 6 = listening card: the two Understand questions; 7 = word card: *vilken, rätt / fel*; 8 = the number pair you mix up most.

**Done?**
- Green: opinion, problem and hope on your own, with *inte* before the verb after *att*; the winter question works too.
- Amber or Red: see the top. Red here: you answered a different question. Watch for ~~att den startar inte~~.

## Day 6: Last weekend

**Audio:** Day 6 in the audio list.

**New patterns (5)**
1. *Förra helgen var jag **hemma**.* Last weekend I was at home. (*förra helgen* last weekend · *var* was; now: *är*) Swap: *i Åbo, i Helsingfors*.
2. *Min **vän** kom på besök.* My friend came to visit. (*min* my · *kom* came; now: *kommer* · *på besök* to visit) Swap: *syster* sister, *bror* brother.
3. *Vi gick på **bio**.* We went to the cinema. (*vi* we · *gick* went; now: *går*) Swap: *museum, konsert*.

5. *Jag vill berätta om min helg.* I want to tell you about my weekend. (*vill* want · *helg* weekend: the opening for a story)

**Words:** *hur* how

4. Type to a friend: where you were, who came, what you did, *Det var …* (15 min)
5. One short clip: write three words you know. (8 min)
6. Cards; send your teacher your two newest recordings and texts. (4 min)

**Cards (7):** 1–5 = patterns 1–5; 6 = listening card: the two Understand questions; 7 = word card: *hur*.

**Done?**

## Day 8: Say no, with a reason

**Audio:** Day 8 in the audio list.

**New patterns (5)**
1. *Tack för inbjudan.* Thanks for the invitation. (*inbjudan* invitation)
2. *Tyvärr kan jag inte komma, eftersom jag **jobbar**.* Unfortunately I can't come, because I'm working. (*eftersom* because)
3. *… eftersom jag inte har tid.* … because I don't have time.
4. *Därför kan jag inte komma.* That's why I can't come. (*därför* that's why; the verb comes next) Before it: *Jag jobbar på fredag.*
5. *Passar det på **söndag**?* Does Sunday suit you? (*passar* suits)

**Words:** *varför* why (it wants a reason, often with *eftersom*) · *förklara* explain

**Rule:** after *eftersom* the person comes first, then *inte*, then the verb: *eftersom jag inte har tid*, not ~~eftersom jag har inte tid~~.

**Steps**

2. Listen and repeat. Ladder: *Jag kan inte komma på fredag.* → pattern 2 → pattern 3 → *Passar det på söndag i stället?* (10 min)

Thank, say why, suggest another day.) Run it (40 s to think, 50 s to speak; then the neighbour's card (back of file), 30 + 40 s). (15 min)
4. Type a reply to your colleague Sara: thanks, no, why, a new day. (15 min)
5. Write each date your helper says, as digits. (8 min)
6. Cards. (4 min)

## Day 9: Yesterday, today, tomorrow

**Audio:** Day 9 in the audio list.

**New patterns (5)**
1. *I går ringde jag till **banken**.* Yesterday I called the bank. (*ringde* called · *till* to)
2. *I dag ringer jag till banken.* Today I'm calling the bank. (*ringer* call)
3. *I morgon ska jag ringa till banken.* Tomorrow I'm going to call the bank. (*ska* + the base form: the future)
4. *I går köpte jag **en ny telefon**.* Yesterday I bought a new phone. (*köpte* bought; now *köper*, later *ska köpa* · *ny* new) Swap: *en ny jacka* a new jacket.
5. *I måndags ringde jag till banken.* Last Monday I called the bank. (*i måndags* last Monday)
Swap the time word: *På måndag ska jag ringa till banken.* Next Monday I'll call the bank. With a Day 6 verb: *I går gick jag på bio.* → *I dag går jag …* → *I morgon ska jag gå …*

**Understand:** *Vad gjorde du i går?* What did you do yesterday? · *Vad ska du göra i morgon?* What are you going to do tomorrow?
**Words:** *hur många* how many · *inställd* cancelled · *försenad* delayed
**Rule:** the time word chooses the verb form: *i går, i måndags* → the past (*ringde*); *i dag* → now (*ringer*); *i morgon, på måndag* → *ska* + base form (*ska ringa*), or the present.

**Steps**
1. Review. (8 min)
2. Listen and repeat. Point back for *i går* and *i måndags*, down for *i dag*, forward for *i morgon* and *på måndag*. Say patterns 1–3 with *gå* and *bio*. (10 min)
3. *Berätta vad du gjorde i går, vad du gör i dag och vad du ska göra i morgon.* Run it (60 s, then 45 s with *i måndags* and *på fredag*). (15 min)
4. Type to a friend: one thing yesterday, one today, one tomorrow. (15 min)
5. On a transport notice, find the times, *inställd* and *försenad*. (8 min)
6. Cards. (4 min)

**Cards (8):** 1–5 = patterns 1–5; 6 = listening card: the two Understand questions; 7 = word card: *hur många*; 8 = word card: *inställd, försenad*.

**Done?**
- Green: yesterday, today and tomorrow, each with its form, on your own; other days work too.
- Amber or Red: see the top. A wrong form that the listener still understands (~~I går ringer jag~~) is Amber.

## Day 10: Describe a lost thing

**Audio:** Day 10 in the audio list.
