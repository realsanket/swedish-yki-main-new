# Swedish YKI: Days 1–14, one page a day

[Unreviewed Swedish: have a teacher check it]

**How to read Green / Amber / Red**
- **Green:** you did the task on your own and gave every point (spelling slips don't count). Go on.
- **Amber:** you got there, but slowly, or one mistake kept coming back. Write it in your error list and go on.
- **Red:** you misunderstood the card, could not answer, or needed to read the model. Do the day again tomorrow with new details.

**Openings and closings: choose them by the situation**

| Situation | Open with | Close with |
|---|---|---|
| Phone call | *Hej, det här är [namn].* | *Tack för hjälpen. Hej då!* |
| Voicemail to a friend | *Hej [namn], det är [namn].* | *Hej då!* |
| Message or e-mail to a friend | *Hej!* | *Hälsningar, [namn]* |
| Formal e-mail | *Hej! Jag skriver angående [___].* | *Med vänliga hälsningar, [namn]* |
| Your opinion (talk or text) | *Jag tycker att [___].* | *Därför tycker jag att [___].* |
| A story or a description (talk) | *Jag vill berätta om [___].* | |
| A request, face to face | *Ursäkta, kan du [___]?* | |
| Reacting to news | *Vad roligt!* / *Vad tråkigt att höra!* | |

Never open a hobby talk with a phone greeting, and never close it like a letter.

**Timers:** the first try has no timer. "Record (40 s)" means a gentle limit; the redo with new details is a little shorter. Times are aims, not tests.

## Day 1: Say who you are

**Hear it first:** each sentence three times (teacher, a Swedish voice or your book's audio), then repeat after the voice.

**Today's sentences**
1. *Jag heter Alex.* My name is Alex.
2. *Jag kommer från Indien.* I come from India.
3. *Jag bor i Esbo.* I live in Espoo.
4. *Jag talar engelska, hindi och marathi.* I speak English, Hindi and Marathi.
5. *Jag lär mig svenska.* I am learning Swedish.

**Understand:** *Vad heter du?* (your name?) · *Var kommer du ifrån?* (where from?) · *Var bor du?* (where do you live?) · *Vilka språk talar du?* (which languages?)

**Words:** *berätta* = tell · *skriv* = write
**Rule:** one verb form for every person: *jag bor, du bor.*

**One step at a time** (no timer today)
1. Listen and repeat. (10 min)
2. Say each sentence from the English, then with a new name, town or language. (10 min)
3. Answer the four questions in full sentences. (10 min)
4. *Berätta om dig själv.* Record your five sentences; redo once. (10 min)
5. Set up å, ä, ö. Type the five sentences from memory; fix. (10 min)
6. Answer as Sara: from Sweden, lives in Åbo, speaks Swedish and Finnish. (5 min)
7. Make 8 cards. Start your error list. (5 min)

**Done?**
- Green: you answer without reading; your text gives all five facts.
- Amber: slowly, or *Jag är bor* came back.
- Red: you misunderstood a question, or needed the model.

**Tomorrow:** your work, your years in Finland, and asking back.
