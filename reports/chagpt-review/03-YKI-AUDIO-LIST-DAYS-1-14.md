# Swedish YKI: the audio list for Days 1–14

[Unreviewed Swedish: have a teacher check it]

*Created on 16 November 2026 (third review). This is the one list a teacher or a voice tool needs to record Days 1–14. It is generated from `YKI-LEARNER-DAYS-1-14.md` and holds every Swedish line in that file, in the order the learner meets it, day by day. Lines already recorded for an earlier day are not repeated, except inside the phone calls, which are given as full scripts.*

**How to record**
- **Clear natural speech first; mumbled or noisy audio only after Day 14.** Record every line clearly, at a normal, natural speed, with a short pause after each line. A slower second take of the patterns is welcome.
- Record each numbered line as its own clip and name it by its number (for example `04-03.mp3`), so the learner can play it three times.
- **Who records it** (one entry per line, in the last column): **Teacher** = your teacher or another Finland-Swedish speaker (first choice for every sentence a learner copies). **Voice** = a Swedish text-to-speech voice is good enough (word lists, number sets, task cards, typed models); the voices available to the project are Sweden-Swedish, so a teacher recording is still better. **Helper, live** = the helper reads the item aloud during the exercise, in a new order; a voice recording is the fallback. **Teacher (or helper)** = the other voice in a phone call.
- **The book's QR audio:** none of these lines. They are original lines written for this course, so they are not in the book; the QR audio belongs to the book's own tasks (which tracks exist has not been checked, see `YKI-BOOK-MAP.md`).
- In a slot line, the words shown are the example to record (for example *Jag heter Alex.*). Struck-through wrong forms on the learner pages are never recorded.
- The phone calls (Days 4, 10, 11 and 12) are full scripts in turn order: record the other voice and the model turns as separate clips, so the learner can also hear the other voice alone and answer it.
