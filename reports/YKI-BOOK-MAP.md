## What the book is

- Speaking and writing practice only (introduction, p6). **No listening or reading material, no answer key, no timing guidance, no assessment criteria.** Listening and reading practice therefore stay in section 7 of the plan.
- 7 themes, 232 entries: Uppvärmning 7, Dialog 34, Reagera 99, Berätta 33, Din åsikt 38, Skriva 21. Speaking: 204 tasks. Writing: 21 tasks (3 per theme).
- Contents page (p5) swaps the names of themes 5 and 6; the page headers are right: p76-90 is *Arbete och utbildning*, p91-116 is *Fritid och hobbyer*.
- About 49 tasks fit none of templates T1-T16 (polite objection, describing a thing/place/habit, comparing home country and Finland, a few one-offs). Templates T17-T20 were written for them, and since 16 November 2026 those 49 rows carry the T17-T20 tag in the Template column. Three rows put another tag first, because of what the task mainly asks: `p52-B4` (a habit talk: T18a, then T19), `p123-BA` (identity: T13, then T19) and `p62-R6` (preferences told to an agent: T2, then T18b).
- Some tasks have print slips (for example p116 task 2 probably lacks the word *inte*). Ask the teacher if a task looks contradictory.
- The book has audio by QR code (SoundCloud playlists on p41, p96, p119, per the catalogue). Since 16 November 2026 it is one of the sources for the "hear it first" step (plan 3.2, rung 0), together with the teacher and other Swedish voices. Which tracks exist, and which tasks they belong to, has not been checked.

## How the book is used in the 84 days

- **Rule (16 November 2026): at most 2 book tasks a day, and a book task replaces a prompt; it is never added on top.** Each one replaces one of the day's own prompts of the nearest kind (a Dialog replaces the call, a Reagera one reaction round or the voicemail, a Berätta or Din åsikt the talk, a Skriva the writing task; a Din åsikt statement can also be the topic of the day's opinion text), or the redo of one. If nothing fits, it waits for a 90-minute day. This follows load rules 5 and 6 in the plan (section 1).
- **Documented exceptions:** the six T17-T20 blocks (Days 22, 24, 30, 31, 34, 38) hold 3 to 5 short tasks each; they are the block's own drill, and the block replaces the day's own speaking block. On Day 48 the three Skriva tasks are the day's three writing prompts (they replace the appendix prompts A6, D6 and F4).
- **Warm-ups (Uppvärmning)** are vocabulary pages, not tasks. They are optional reading on a 90-minute or light day.
- **Days 1-14:** only the vocabulary warm-ups of the first themes, and a few easy items. The book tasks are exam-style and need the templates first.
- **Days 15-42:** book dialogues and reactions, placed on or just after the day their template is taught; Skriva tasks on matching writing days; Din åsikt statements on opinion days.
- **Days 43-55:** each topic day takes its matching book theme (1 me/home, 2 shops/services, 3 environment, 4 health, 5 work, 6 culture/travel, 7 society).
- **Days 64-82:** few book tasks, because most of their old tasks are now **held back for mocks** (40 tasks, see below). Day 82 has none. Never on rest or light days, mock days (28, 56, 84) or exit-test days (14, 41, 42, 62, 63).
- **Question bank (71 tasks):** the tasks cut to keep each day at 1-2. They are not scheduled. Use them on a 90-minute day, on a repeat day (load rules 8 and 9), and on the rotation days of the cycle after Day 84.
- **Seen and fresh.** A book task becomes seen practice once you have done it, or read it closely. Only the held-back tasks stay fresh for the mocks that count towards booking.
