# Practice book map: every task in *Förbered dig för allmän språkexamen* and the day it is used

*Companion to `YKI-EXAM-FIRST-PLAN.md` and `YKI-EXAM-FIRST-CURRICULUM.md`. The book (Gimara Oy, 2022, repaired 2025, 126 pages) is © Gimara Oy, so this file holds **page pointers and our own short descriptions only**, never the book's text. Template IDs T1-T16 are in the plan; T17-T20 are in `YKI-NEW-TEMPLATES-T17-T20.md`.*

## What the book is

- Speaking and writing practice only (introduction, p6). **No listening or reading material, no answer key, no timing guidance, no assessment criteria.** Listening and reading practice therefore stay in section 7 of the plan.
- 7 themes, 232 entries: Uppvärmning 7, Dialog 34, Reagera 99, Berätta 33, Din åsikt 38, Skriva 21. Speaking: 204 tasks. Writing: 21 tasks (3 per theme).
- Contents page (p5) swaps the names of themes 5 and 6; the page headers are right: p76-90 is *Arbete och utbildning*, p91-116 is *Fritid och hobbyer*.
- About 49 tasks fit none of templates T1-T16 (polite objection, describing a thing/place/habit, comparing home country and Finland, a few one-offs). Templates T17-T20 were written for them.
- Some tasks have print slips (for example p116 task 2 probably lacks the word *inte*). Ask the teacher if a task looks contradictory.
- The book has audio by QR code (SoundCloud playlists on p41, p96, p119, per the catalogue). Not used in this plan.

## How the book is used in the 84 days

- **Days 1-14:** only the vocabulary warm-ups of the first themes, and a few easy items. The book tasks are exam-style and need the templates first.
- **Days 15-42:** book dialogues and reactions, placed on or just after the day their template is taught; Skriva tasks on matching writing days; Din åsikt statements on opinion days.
- **Days 43-55:** each topic day takes its matching book theme (1 me/home, 2 shops/services, 3 environment, 4 health, 5 work, 6 culture/travel, 7 society).
- **Days 64-82:** leftover tasks as extra unseen prompts. Never on rest or light days, mock days (28, 56, 84) or exit-test days (14, 41, 42, 62, 63).
- 1 to 5 book tasks per study day. Where a book writing task is listed, it replaces the day's own writing prompt unless you have a 90-minute day.
