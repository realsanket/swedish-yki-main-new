# Reaching Swedish YKI Level 3 From Zero

**Bottom line.** Level 3 on the Swedish-language YKI intermediate test (CEFR B1) is the pass grade, and it is graded separately for each of four subtests (listening, speaking, reading, writing); OPH describes no overall grade, and no compensation between subtests is described ([OPH, After the YKI test](https://www.oph.fi/en/education-and-qualifications/after-yki-test)). If your purpose is Finnish citizenship, you do not need level 3 in all four: you need grade 3 or 4 in one of three pairs (speaking+writing, listening+writing, or reading+speaking), and the same pairs apply to permanent residence ([Migri, YKI](https://migri.fi/en/national-certificate-of-language-proficiency-yki-); [Migri, language skills requirement](https://migri.fi/en/language-skills-requirement)). Your purpose, exam date and daily study hours were left blank, so this report gives scenario plans and does not assume citizenship. Today (14 Nov 2026) is the last Swedish sitting of 2026 and registration is closed; the remaining Swedish intermediate sittings in the notes are all in spring 2027 and all digital: 23 Jan (full), 3 Mar (writing + listening only), 17 Apr (full) and 19 May (speaking + reading only) ([OPH, test days](https://www.oph.fi/sv/utbildning-och-examina/att-valja-ett-lampligt-yki-test-testdagarna)). The honest feasibility call is that no 4-to-24-week plan from A0 is supported by the evidence: the only two anchors found put A0 to B1 at roughly **350 to 960 total hours** (Cambridge's 350-400 guided hours for B1 and the US State Department's Swedish estimate of 552 class hours plus 17 self-study hours a week for 24 weeks), and even 24 weeks at 120 minutes a day is only about 336 hours ([Cambridge English](https://support.cambridgeenglish.org/hc/en-gb/articles/202838506-Guided-learning-hours); [US State Department](https://www.state.gov/foreign-language-training/)). The 350-960 range is my inference from two non-Swedish-YKI anchors, not a measured timeline. The most defensible strategy is therefore to target one legally accepted subtest pair, use the subtest-only days (3 Mar and 19 May 2027) to do it, and treat a spring 2027 sitting as a diagnostic-or-stretch goal rather than an expected pass. Because registrations are binding and refunds are very narrow since 1 Jan 2026, book only after a diagnostic ([OPH news, 12 Dec 2025](https://www.oph.fi/en/news/2025/changes-yki-tests-1-january-2026)).

## Part 2 — Exactly how level 3 is reached

### 2.1 What level 3 means, in plain English

Level 3 is the first pass grade on the intermediate test. In everyday words, it means you can manage ordinary practical situations in Swedish and be understood even when your language is clearly imperfect. The official 2011 examination basis puts it this way for each skill (translated from the Swedish quotes in [the exam basis](https://www.oph.fi/sites/default/files/documents/ykiperusteetruo2011.pdf)) **[Official]:**

- **Listening:** understands longer stretches of speech if the subject is fairly familiar (clear, normal-pace speech on familiar topics; long sequences or unfamiliar topics cause difficulty).
- **Speaking:** copes in usual practical speaking situations and can take initiative in everyday communication; is understood despite flaws in language and/or pronunciation.
- **Reading:** understands usual texts that do not require knowledge of the subject.
- **Writing:** can write simple, unified texts about usual subjects; some unclear expressions, inconsistencies or non-target structures sometimes disturb reading.

The YKI speaking and writing criteria documents, which apply to all test languages and not specifically Swedish, add detail ([speaking](https://www.jyu.fi/en/file-download/download/public/14098); [writing](https://www.jyu.fi/en/file-download/download/public/14097); one note cites the writing PDF as file 14099, so check both numbers if a link fails) **[Official]:**

| | Level 2 (below pass) | **Level 3 (pass)** | Level 4 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Speaking** | Only routine, simple exchanges; frequent pauses; strong foreign accent; relies on the other speaker; basic structures often wrong | Copes with most practical everyday situations and takes initiative; speech may be fairly slow with some unnatural pauses, especially in longer turns; first-language transfer allowed; pronunciation may be clearly non-target but understood; limited register shifts; linking may be limited and repetitive, but ideas join into a coherent whole; errors mainly on complex or unfamiliar topics; structures target-like in familiar situations, awkward in demanding ones, yet the message comes across | Copes fairly well in less familiar situations; distinguishes formal and informal register; fairly fluent; relatively accurate structures |

### 2.2 Reporting, compensation and retakes

**One result or several?** Four separate grades, one per subtest; no single overall result, and the certificate lists the subtests completed ([OPH, selecting the test](https://www.oph.fi/en/education-and-qualifications/selecting-right-yki-test-test-days)) **[Official]**. **Compensation:** none described; the only "compensation" is legal: the three accepted pairs let one weak skill be tolerated if the other pair is strong **[Inference]**. **Which combinations satisfy a purpose:** see Part 0. **Retaking individual subtests:** the notes found no stated retake limit or waiting period (**not found**); the practical route is a subtest-only day (3 Mar and 19 May 2027 for Swedish), and certificates issued after 1 Jan 2012 may be combined for citizenship and permanent residence **[Inference for the practical route]**. The same-day conflict in Part 0 applies here too. A "not met" on one subtest does not reject the whole test ([OPH, after the test](https://www.oph.fi/en/education-and-qualifications/after-yki-test)).

**How scores are made.** Trained raters assess speaking and writing against the criteria, not against other candidates; the rating comes from the performance as a whole, "not from any single criterion" ([exam basis](https://www.oph.fi/sites/default/files/documents/ykiperusteetruo2011.pdf); [JYU criteria](https://www.jyu.fi/en/file-download/download/public/14098)). No raw-score "points", cut scores or category weights are published for Swedish: **not found**. How listening and reading answers convert to levels: **not found**. Do not trust anyone who quotes you a pass mark.

### 2.5 The minimum viable language question

**What must you genuinely develop for level 3, and what can wait?** This is my classification from the criteria and sample tasks **[Expert estimate]**:

| Bucket | Content |
|---|---|
| **Essential for level 3** | Understanding the Swedish task instructions and question words; present tense of common verbs; main-clause word order (verb second); negation with *inte*; modal verbs (*kan, vill, måste, ska*) + infinitive; en/ett nouns and common plurals; personal pronouns and possessives; common prepositions (*på, i, till, från, med, om*); numbers, times, dates, prices; core everyday vocabulary (people, home, work, shops, health, transport); polite phone and letter openings and closings; connectors *och, men, eftersom, så, att*; simple past (*gick, åt, hade, var*) and *har* + past participle for "what happened"; question forms |

### 4.1 What the evidence allows

No official page in the notes says whether prepared or memorised answers are allowed, tolerated or penalised: **not found** ([JYU speaking criteria](https://www.jyu.fi/en/file-download/download/public/14098) and [writing criteria](https://www.jyu.fi/en/file-download/download/public/14097) are silent on them, as are the OPH test-day instructions). ## Part 5 — Which grammar is actually worth learning

### 5.1 Plain-English glossary

A **verb** is an action or state word (*bor* = lives). A **noun** names a thing (*bil* = car). The **subject** is who or what does the verb (*jag*, *bussen*). The **infinitive** is the base form with *-a* ("to ...", *arbeta*). **Tense** shows time (present, past, future). A **clause** is a group of words with a subject and a verb. A **main clause** can stand alone; a **subordinate clause** cannot (it starts with a joining word such as *att* or *eftersom*). **Word order** is the order of these parts. A **preposition** is a small word for place, time or direction (*på, i, till*). An **adjective** describes a noun (*stor* = big). A **pronoun** replaces a noun (*jag, du, hon*).

**Evidence note.** No official Swedish B1 grammar checklist or error-frequency study was found ([notes: resources_feasibility, section c]). The official level 3 descriptors are communicative and say structures are target-like in familiar situations and awkward in demanding ones but the message comes across ([JYU speaking criteria](https://www.jyu.fi/en/file-download/download/public/14098)). The tiers below are therefore **my expert ordering, based on those descriptors and general knowledge of Swedish**, not an official ranking. Finland-Swedish and Sweden-Swedish share the same standard grammar; the varieties differ mainly in pronunciation and some vocabulary, so grammar study can use either (blog source, anecdotal, [Figuring Out Finland](https://figuringoutfinland.substack.com/p/swedish-in-finland-should-you-learn)).

| Tier | Meaning | Items |
|---|---|---|
| **A: learn immediately** | Needed to understand tasks and communicate basic meaning | 1 word order, 2 present tense, 3 pronouns, 4 questions, 5 negation, 6 modal verbs, 7 en/ett and plurals, 8 prepositions, 14 time and numbers |
| **B: raise the chance of level 3** | Clarity and range across tasks | 9 past, 10 future, 11 connecting ideas and subordinate clauses, 12 reasons, 13 comparisons and adjectives |
| **C: if time permits** | Refinements | 15 reflexive and particle verbs, 16 relative clauses with *som* |
| **D: defer** | Low immediate value | 17 passive, and subjunctive, pluperfect, advanced *sin/sitt*, idioms |

### 5.2 The items

**1. Basic sentence order, with the verb in second place (V2) — Tier A — value HIGH.** In an ordinary statement the verb is always the second "piece". The first piece can be the subject or something else (a time or place); if it is something else, the subject moves *after* the verb. Examples: *Jag bor i Esbo.* (I live in Espoo.) *Nu bor jag i Esbo.* (Now I live in Espoo.) *I morgon har jag tid.* (Tomorrow I have time.) *På fredag går jag till läkaren.* (On Friday I go to the doctor.) *Tyvärr kan jag inte komma.* (Unfortunately I cannot come.) *How it helps:* every task is built from statements; correct V2 after time words like *i går* and *tyvärr* shows control and prevents confusing sentences. *Beginner mistake:* copying English or Indian-language order after a time word: *I morgon jag har tid* (wrong).

**2. Present tense — Tier A — HIGH.** One verb form is used for every person (*jag, du, han, vi, de* all share it). Most verbs end in *-ar*, *-er* or *-r*. Examples: *Jag arbetar på ett kafé.* (I work in a café.) *Hon läser en bok.* (She reads a book.) *Vi bor i Vanda.* (We live in Vantaa.) *De har två barn.* (They have two children.) *Du är sen.* (You are late.) *How it helps:* most speaking and writing answers about "now" and habits. *Mistake:* adding person endings (*jag arbetade* means past); or using two verbs (*jag är arbetar*).

**3. Pronouns and possessives — Tier A — HIGH.** Subject forms: *jag, du, han, hon, vi, ni, de*; object forms (after verbs/prepositions): *mig, dig, honom, henne, oss, er, dem*. "My" is *min* (en-words), *mitt* (ett-words), *mina* (plural). Examples: *Jag ringer dig i kväll.* (I will call you tonight.) *Hon hjälper mig.* (She helps me.) *Det här är min syster.* (This is my sister.) *Mitt hus är litet.* (My house is small.) *Hennes man arbetar här.* (Her husband works here.) *How it helps:* describing people, phone calls, complaints. *Mistake:* *Hon hjälper jag* (wrong object form); *min* with ett-words.

**4. Questions — Tier A — HIGH.** A yes/no question puts the verb first. A question with a question word puts the word first, then the verb. Question words: *vad, vem, var, när, hur, varför, vilken, hur mycket*. Examples: *Har du tid i morgon?* (Do you have time tomorrow?) *Vad heter du?* (What is your name?) *Var bor du?* (Where do you live?) *När öppnar affären?* (When does the shop open?) *Hur mycket kostar den?* (How much does it cost?) *How it helps:* understanding task questions; role-play phone questions. *Mistake:* using "do": *Gör du bor här?* (wrong).

**5. Negation — Tier A — HIGH.** In a main clause *inte* (not) comes right after the verb. For "no + noun" use *ingen* (en-word), *inget* (ett-word), *inga* (plural). Examples: *Jag har inte tid.* (I have no time.) *Jag kan inte komma.* (I can't come.) *Hon bor inte här.* (She doesn't live here.) *Jag har ingen bil.* (I have no car.) *Vi har inga problem.* (We have no problems.) *How it helps:* understanding negative statements in listening and reading ("did *not*") and making complaints ("it still does *not* work"). *Mistake:* *Jag inte kan komma* (wrong in a main clause; it is correct inside a subordinate clause, item 11).

**6. Modal verbs — Tier A — HIGH.** Modals are helper verbs: *kan* (can), *vill* (want), *måste* (must), *ska* (will/going to/shall), *får* (may/am allowed to). The next verb stays in the infinitive, with no *att*. Examples: *Jag kan hjälpa dig.* (I can help you.) *Jag vill boka en tid.* (I want to book an appointment.) *Du måste betala fakturan.* (You must pay the bill.) *Vi ska träffas klockan fem.* (We are going to meet at five.) *Får jag ställa en fråga?* (May I ask a question?) *How it helps:* requests, complaints, offers and invitations all run on these. *Mistake:* *Jag kan att hjälpa*; *Jag kan hjälper*.

**7. En/ett words, "the" endings and plurals — Tier A — HIGH for recognising, medium for perfection.** Every noun is an en-word or an ett-word; most are en-words. "The" is added as an ending: *-en/-n* for en-words, *-et/-t* for ett-words. Plural endings vary, so learn each noun with its article. Examples: *en butik – butiken – butiker* (a shop – the shop – shops); *ett kök – köket – kök* (a kitchen); *en faktura – fakturan – fakturor* (a bill); *ett möte – mötet – möten* (a meeting); *ett barn – barnet – barn* (a child). *How it helps:* nearly every sentence has a noun. Gender errors rarely block meaning, so do not spend weeks on perfection **[Expert estimate]**. *Mistake:* using *en* for every noun; saying *den bilen* when *bilen* is enough.

**8. Common prepositions — Tier A — HIGH (learn as chunks).** *på* (on/at), *i* (in), *till* (to), *från* (from), *med* (with), *om* (about; also "in" for time ahead), *för* (for), *hos* (at someone's place), *efter/före* (after/before). Use them in fixed phrases. Examples: *Jag bor i Helsingfors.* (I live in Helsinki.) *Jag arbetar på ett sjukhus.* (I work at a hospital.) *Jag åker till jobbet med buss.* (I go to work by bus.) *Bussen går från stationen.* (The bus leaves from the station.) *Vi ses om en timme.* (We'll meet in an hour.) *How it helps:* places, times, directions in calls and letters. *Mistake:* translating English word for word; learn *på måndag, i juli, på kvällen, till exempel* as units.

**9. Past tense and "har" + past form — Tier B — HIGH.** Two ways to talk about the past. The simple past (*arbetade, bodde, gick, åt, var, hade, fick*) is for finished events with a time. The perfect uses *har* + a past-participle-like form (*har arbetat, har bott, har gått*) for events connected to now or without a time. Examples: *I går arbetade jag hemma.* (Yesterday I worked at home.) *Vi åt middag klockan sju.* (We ate dinner at seven.) *Jag har bott i Finland i två år.* (I have lived in Finland for two years.) *Bussen var sen.* (The bus was late.) *Har du ringt dem?* (Have you called them?) *How it helps:* describing a person you have known, explaining what happened, complaining that "nothing has been repaired". *Mistake:* using present for past; leaving out *har* (*Jag bott här*).

**10. Future — Tier B — MEDIUM-HIGH.** Use *ska* + infinitive, *kommer att* + infinitive, or the present tense with a time word. Examples: *Jag ska träffa en vän i morgon.* (I'm going to meet a friend tomorrow.) *Vi kommer att flytta nästa år.* (We will move next year.) *Bussen går om fem minuter.* (The bus leaves in five minutes.) *Hon ska ringa dig senare.* (She will call you later.) *Det kommer att regna.* (It is going to rain.) *How it helps:* plans, promises and phone arrangements. *Mistake:* *Jag vill ringa dig imorgon* when you mean "will".

**11. Joining ideas, and subordinate-clause order — Tier B — HIGH.** *och, men, eller, så* join two full sentences without changing the order. *att* (that), *eftersom* (because), *när* (when), *om* (if/whether), *som* (who/that) start a subordinate clause, and there *inte* goes *before* the verb. Examples: *Jag vill komma, men jag har ingen tid.* (I want to come, but I have no time.) *Jag stannar hemma eftersom jag är sjuk.* (I'm staying home because I am sick.) *Jag tror att du gillar jazz.* (I think you like jazz.) *Jag vet att han inte kommer.* (I know he is not coming.) *Jag ringer dig när jag är hemma.* (I'll call you when I am home.) *How it helps:* coherent text and the "linking" criterion. *Mistake:* *Jag vet att han kommer inte* (wrong). At level 3, an error here rarely stops understanding, so learn the rule but accept slips.

**12. Giving reasons — Tier B — HIGH.** *eftersom / därför att* (because), *för att* (in order to), *därför* (so/therefore; the verb comes straight after: *Därför kan jag inte komma*), *så* (so). Examples: *Jag kan inte komma, eftersom jag arbetar.* (I cannot come because I am working.) *Jag lär mig svenska för att få medborgarskap.* (I am learning Swedish to get citizenship.) *Jag arbetar på kvällen. Därför kan jag inte komma.* (I work in the evening. So I can't come.) *Fakturan är för hög, så jag vill ha en förklaring.* (The bill is too high, so I want an explanation.) *How it helps:* the letter needs three reasons; the complaint needs two. *Mistake:* *för* instead of *för att* when you mean purpose; *Därför jag kan inte* (wrong).

**13. Adjectives and comparisons — Tier B — MEDIUM.** Adjectives change with the noun: *en stor bil, ett stort hus, stora bilar*. Compare with *-are* and *-ast*, or *mer/mest*: *snabb – snabbare – snabbast*. Irregular: *bra – bättre – bäst; dålig – sämre – sämst; många – fler – flest*. Examples: *Bussen är billigare än taxi.* (The bus is cheaper than a taxi.) *Det här huset är större än det där.* (This house is bigger than that one.) *Det är den bästa butiken.* (It is the best shop.) *Tåget är snabbare men dyrare.* (The train is faster but more expensive.) *Det är lika dyrt som förra året.* (It is as expensive as last year.) *How it helps:* opinions, advantages and disadvantages, complaints about price. *Mistake:* *mer billig* (use *billigare*).

**14. Time, dates and numbers — Tier A — HIGH (but it is vocabulary practice, not rules).** The big trap: *halv tre* means **2:30**, not 3:30. Examples: *Klockan är halv sju.* (It is 6:30.) *Vi ses klockan fem.* (We'll meet at five.) *Mötet börjar kvart över nio.* (The meeting starts at 9:15.) *Det kostar tjugofem euro.* (It costs 25 euros.) *Min födelsedag är den 4 juli.* (My birthday is 4 July.) *How it helps:* proposing a time, giving an amount in a complaint, number-heavy reading, listening for details. *Mistake:* mixing *halv* and *över*; confusing *femton* and *femtio*.

**15. Reflexive and particle verbs — Tier C — MEDIUM-LOW.** Reflexive verbs need *mig/dig/sig* (*Jag känner mig trött.* I feel tired). Particle verbs add a small word (*ringa upp* = phone; *Jag ringer upp dig.*). Others: *Hon klär på sig.* (She gets dressed.) *Vi tar emot gäster.* (We receive guests.) Learn only the common ones as chunks.

**16. Relative clauses with *som* — Tier C — MEDIUM.** *som* means "who/which/that" and joins a description: *Mannen som bor här är min granne.* (The man who lives here is my neighbour.) *Det är en butik som säljer cyklar.* (It is a shop that sells bikes.) Useful for explaining a missing word, but you can avoid it with two short sentences.

**17. The *s*-passive — Tier D — LOW for production; recognise it in reading.** *Fakturan skickades i går.* (The bill was sent yesterday.) *Butiken öppnas klockan nio.* (The shop opens at nine.)

**Defer (Tier D):** the subjunctive, pluperfect (*hade gått*), advanced *sin/sitt*, subordinate-clause inversions, long relative chains, fine points of definite forms, and most idioms. The level 3 descriptors do not require them.

## Part 6 — Section-by-section tactics

Rules that cover every subtest, from OPH and the practice-site instructions ([OPH, before the test](https://www.oph.fi/sv/utbildning-och-examina/fore-yki-testdagen); [JYU instructions](https://ykitesti2.solki.jyu.fi/ykitesti2/web/en/node/2)) **[Official]:** read and listen to the instructions carefully and follow them; try to answer every question and task; you must complete all subtests you registered for to receive a certificate (an unfinished subtest cannot be assessed). I do not know, and no source states, how many marks any individual item is worth, so none of the tactics below rest on mark values.

### 6A. Listening

**What is verified.** Three task types in the intermediate listening sample: multiple choice, true/false, and open questions ([JYU listening sample](https://ykitesti2.solki.jyu.fi/ykitesti2/web/en/tutustu-testiin/svenska/mellanniva-talforstaelse/)); the sample has three audio files, each tied to one exercise. **What is not verified:** how many times audio plays (one research note says twice, but a second note that tried the same page could not confirm it); whether questions can be read before listening; whether question order follows the audio; item counts; the speakers' varieties (Finland or Sweden Swedish); typical distractors. A single anecdote from a 2022 forum post says the listening used Finland-Swedish and was "very basic and clear" ([hotplay, Finland Forum](https://www.finlandforum.org/viewtopic.php?p=600017) **[Anecdotal]**). Another candidate found listening the hardest because Swedish is rarely heard in daily life in Helsinki ([Online Swedish testimonial](https://onlineswedish.org/success-story/yki-test-finnish-citizenship-yki-preparation/) **[Anecdotal, marketing]**). The two accounts conflict, so neither supports a claim about difficulty.

**Strategies (all coaching advice, not official):**
- *Read the question first, efficiently.* Underline mentally the question word: *vem* (who), *vad* (what), *var* (where), *när* (when), *hur många* (how many), *varför* (why). Decide which type of word you are listening for.
- *Listen for keywords by type.* Numbers, dates, times, prices and places are the cheapest points: practise *tjugo/tjugotvå, trettio/trettiotre, halv tre, kvart över, på måndag, den 12 mars*.
- *Negatives and changes.* Listen for *inte, aldrig, ingen, utan, men, i stället, tyvärr, ändrat, flyttat, inställt* (cancelled). These words often reverse the first thing you heard **[Expert estimate]**.
- *If you miss part.* Do not freeze. Mark your best guess and move to the next item. One lost question should not cost the next one. Wrong answers carry no penalty in the reading sample (official statement for reading; for listening **not found**), so never leave an item blank.
- *Unknown word.* Skip it; listen to what comes before and after, and answer from the whole idea.
- *Open questions.* Answer with the shortest correct Swedish: one to four words that you heard. Spelling can be rough; the meaning must be clear.

**Step-by-step listening routine (adaptable to unknown playback rules):**
1. Read the instruction line and every question you can see; note the question words.
2. First listening: decide topic, who is speaking, and what they want (gist).
3. Answer the easy items immediately (numbers, names, places).
4. If there is a second play, use it only on the items you left uncertain.
5. For each item, check for a negative or a change of plan before you commit.
6. Answer every item; move on after about the time the instructions allow (the sample shows 15-30 second answer windows per item, **unverified**).

**How to use real audio without memorising transcripts.** Use Finland-Swedish audio that matches exam contexts: *Yle Nyheter på lätt svenska*, published every weekday with sound and text ([Luckan, News in Swedish](https://integration.luckan.fi/integratinginswedish/news-in-swedish/); [Yle Arenan](https://arenan.yle.fi/1-50362086)) **[Official/NGO]**. For each 10-minute session: (1) listen once without text and write three facts you caught; (2) listen again and write all numbers and names; (3) read the transcript, mark words you could not hear, and say them aloud; (4) next day, listen to the *same* audio first, then a *new* clip. Memorising a transcript trains recognition of one clip; the next day's new clip tests whether you can really hear Swedish.

**Listening scenarios.** Ten original practice scenarios with transcripts, questions, answers and explanations are in **Appendix D**. They are **original practice material, not official**, because the official sample items could not be read.

### 6B. Reading

**What is verified.** Three task types (multiple choice, true/false, open questions); answer every question; wrong answers carry no penalty; only one option per multiple-choice question is correct; paper instructions say MC and T/F answers go on a separate answer sheet and only that sheet counts; open answers are handwritten in the booklet ([JYU reading sample](https://ykitesti2.solki.jyu.fi/ykitesti2/web/en/tutustu-testiin/svenska/mellanniva-textforstaelse/)) **[Official]**. Observed text types: advertisement, feature article with figures, news report. **Not found:** time limit, how digital answers are recorded, the questions themselves, distractors. The digital spring 2027 test may not use a separate answer sheet ([notes: listening_reading_tasks]) **[Inference]**.

**Tactics** **[Coaching advice]:**
- *Skim first.* Read the title, the first line and any subheadings; decide the text type and topic in under 20 seconds.
- *Scan for keywords.* Find the question's keyword in the text (names, numbers, dates, places), then read only the sentence around it.
- *Paraphrase and synonyms.* Exams rarely repeat the exact words. Learn pairs such as *billig/prisvärd, gratis/utan kostnad, öppet/tillgängligt, ringa/kontakta*.
- *Practical documents.* Know the layout of advertisements (headline, benefits, price, contact), notices (what, when, where, who) and e-mails (greeting, request, deadline, signature).
- *Eliminating options.* With one right answer and no penalty, eliminate options that contradict the text or add facts the text does not mention; then guess among those left.
- *Unknown words.* Guess from context or from a similar English word (*information, problem, kontakt, service* are close to English); do not stop to translate.
- *Time.* Because the real time limit is unknown, practise at an even pace: if you have a total time T for N items, spend no more than T/N on any item at first reading and return to skipped ones at the end.
- *Number-heavy texts.* Reading text 2 in the sample is full of registration figures; practise finding the right number *and* its label (what the number counts).

Ten original reading exercises with answers are in **Appendix E**; they are original practice material, not official, and imitate the three observed text types.

### 6C. Speaking

**What is verified.** Four sample items: a 1-minute preparation (paper notes allowed) then 1.5 minutes about a person important to you; a phone call from a buyer answering your "Säljes" ad, with turns of 10-25 seconds (the notes disagree on whether the sample has 7 or 8 turns; the old page lists 7, the newer page 8); a phone call to a repair company about an unrepaired television, 6 turns of 10-25 seconds; and a 20-second voicemail saying you will be late ([JYU speaking sample, old](https://ykitesti.solki.jyu.fi/tutustu-testiin/svenska/mellanniva-talproduktion/); [new](https://ykitesti2.solki.jyu.fi/ykitesti2/web/en/tutustu-testiin/svenska/mellanniva-talproduktion/)) **[Official sample, not a guaranteed current format]**. The other speaker is a pre-recorded voice; your answers are recorded and rated later. A 2022 forum poster describes speaking into a computer in a room of about 30 candidates, noisy enough that they suggest requesting special arrangements ([hotplay](https://www.finlandforum.org/viewtopic.php?p=600017) **[Anecdotal, 2022, paper/earlier format]**). Digital speaking mechanics (headset, on-screen timer) are **not found**. Preparation time for tasks other than the monologue is not stated. Whether you can restart a response: **not found**.

**What level 3 requires in practice.** From the speaking criteria: be understood; manage everyday practical situations; take initiative; join ideas; accept non-native pronunciation and slow speech with some pauses ([JYU speaking criteria](https://www.jyu.fi/en/file-download/download/public/14098)). In the sample, "taking initiative" shows up in task prompts such as "suggest a time and say where you live" and "protest and explain what still does not work".

**How to start without freezing** **[Coaching advice]:**
1. *Use a fixed first sentence.* For phone calls: *Hej, det här är [namn].* For a story: *Jag vill berätta om...* For voicemail: *Hej [namn], det är [namn].* A first sentence you can say without thinking buys you three seconds to plan the second.
2. *Answer the question immediately.* Say the main point in the first sentence, then add reasons. For example: *Jag kan tyvärr inte komma, eftersom jag arbetar.* before any story.
3. *Use short sentences.* Subject + verb + object, five to eight words. Do not translate long English sentences.
4. *Add one reason and one example to every answer* (*eftersom...*, *till exempel...*). This lifts a short answer to a developed one without new grammar.
5. *If you make a mistake, correct it once and go on.* *Förlåt, jag menar...* Do not restart whole answers; time is short.
6. *Stop when the task is covered.* Do not pad with irrelevant sentences. The sample advises keeping talking in the 1.5-minute monologue; the way to do this without padding is to cover each of the six suggested content points (who the person is and why important; what they are like; how long you have known them; the best thing about them; what you do together; what you would like to tell them) for 12-15 seconds each.

**When you think in English but cannot say it in Swedish.** Use the *ladder*:
1. Say the simplest true thing you *can* say in Swedish instead (*Jag är arg* rather than "I am deeply disappointed").
2. Replace the missing word with a describing phrase (#4-5 in the phrase table).
3. Use a word you know plus *inte* or *mycket* (*inte bra* for "terrible").
4. Use a Swedish-English cognate and say it in Swedish pronunciation (*problem, information, service, kontakt*).
5. Never switch to English or Hindi/Marathi; Swedish tasks must be answered in Swedish ([OPH, before the test](https://www.oph.fi/sv/utbildning-och-examina/fore-yki-testdagen)).

**Timed drills (all original practice drills):**
- **Drill S1, monologue:** 1 minute prepare with paper notes (6 keywords), then 1.5 minutes. Record. Topic bank: Appendix A and B.
- **Drill S2, phone turns:** use a timer; a friend or an AI reads the other line; answer within 10, 20, 25 seconds; no repeats. Record.
- **Drill S3, voicemail:** 20-second message; ask yourself three questions first (who am I, what happened, what do I need).
- **Review rule:** listen to the recording once for content (did I answer every point?) and once for sound (was I understandable?).

Thirty original speaking prompts, each with outline, simple model answer, English translation, reusable expressions, likely errors and a harder follow-up, are in **Appendix B**. Their task types are marked **V** (modelled on a verified sample type) or **I** (inferred, plausible everyday situation).

### 6D. Writing

**What is verified.** Three tasks: an informal e-mail about an event for a friend (what, when, where + two extra details; open and close appropriately); a letter to the editor on one of two themes ("Det är viktigt att lära barn pengars värde" or "Dagens mode är inte snyggt") giving the topic, your opinion and at least three reasons; and a formal complaint about a kitchen-renovation bill: which bill, at least two reasons it is unreasonable, a suitable amount, and contact details. The paper test is handwritten ("Skriv med tydlig handstil") ([JYU writing sample](https://ykitesti2.solki.jyu.fi/ykitesti2/web/en/tutustu-testiin/svenska/mellanniva-skriftlig-framstallning/)) **[Official sample]**. **Not found:** word counts, time limit, digital input (typed or handwritten), number of tasks in the current test (the 2011 basis says three texts).

**Response structure for each task type:**

| Task | Structure (sentences) |
|---|---|
| **Informal message** | Greeting; reason for writing (1); *what* the event is (1); *when* (1); *where* (1); two extra details (2); invitation or question (1); closing (1). About 8-10 short sentences |
| **Letter to editor** | Topic and opinion (1-2); reason 1 with example (2); reason 2 with example (2); reason 3 with example (2); conclusion (1); closing. About 9-12 sentences |
| **Formal complaint** | Greeting (*Hej,* or *Bästa mottagare,*); which bill (number, date, amount) (1-2); what is wrong: reason 1 (1-2), reason 2 (1-2); what you want: suitable amount (1); contact details (1); polite closing (*Med vänliga hälsningar, [namn]*). About 8-10 sentences |

**Does length matter?** No official word count exists. The criteria reward *content and clarity*, not length, and missing required points is a risk because the instructions spell them out **[Inference]**. A short but complete answer can meet the content requirements; a long answer that omits a required point may not. Handwriting must be readable on paper.

**Learning to tell four kinds of response apart** (all original examples; the task is the complaint):

| Type | Example | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Too incomplete** | "Hej. Fakturan är dyr. Hej då." (Hi. The bill is expensive. Bye.) | No bill number, no reasons, no amount, no contact details. Fails the content checklist |
| **Mistakes but understandable** | "Hej. Jag fick faktura 123 för 5 000 euro. Det är för mycket, för jag betalar inte så mycket för kök. Jag vill betala 3 000 euro. Min telefon är 040 123 4567." (I got bill 123 for 5,000 euros. It's too much, because I pay not so much for kitchen. I want pay 3,000 euros...) | Covers bill, one weak reason, amount, contact; word choice and grammar are shaky, but the reader understands. Needs a second clear reason |
| **Covers the task reasonably well** | "Hej! Jag skriver om faktura 123 från den 2 mars på 5 000 euro. Fakturan är för hög av två skäl. För det första var arbetet färdigt först efter åtta veckor, inte efter fem som ni lovade. För det andra har ni tagit betalt för material som jag inte har fått. Jag tycker att 3 500 euro är rimligt. Ni når mig på 040 123 4567. Med vänliga hälsningar, Rahul Sharma" | All points, simple correct structures, formal closing |
| **Unnecessarily complicated** | "Jag tillåter mig härmed att på det allvarligaste vis invända mot den fakturerade summan, vilken uppenbarligen står i missförhållande till de utförda tjänsterna..." (I hereby allow myself to object in the gravest manner...) | Long formal phrases are risky for a beginner, may be wrong, and do not add points the criteria value at level 3. Simple clear Swedish is better |

**Maximising task completion and clarity** **[Coaching advice]:** (1) copy the task's required points into a checklist before writing; (2) write one clear sentence for each point; (3) use *För det första / För det andra / För det tredje* to make three reasons visible; (4) reread for one thing only, "did I answer every point?"; (5) then check verbs (second place), *inte*, and en/ett only for the top few repeated words; (6) write the greeting and the closing the register needs (friend: *Hej, kram*; company: *Bästa mottagare, Med vänliga hälsningar*).

## Part 9 — The minimum viable Swedish toolkit

The word lists and patterns requested in the master prompt are in **Appendix F**: the top 100 essential words (A), the next 200 useful words grouped by theme (B), the top 50 verbs (C), the top 50 reusable expressions (D), and 30 high-value sentence patterns (E). Each pattern has Swedish, simple English meaning, a short example, typical use and priority. **They are my own selection, built from the verified task types and OPH topic areas, not from a frequency count of the exam.**

**How much is enough?** 300 words are **not** enough for robust level 3 performance. The list is a starting study sequence for the first weeks, not a threshold. For scale: the Swedish Kelly list holds 8,425 lemmas that cover 80% of a web corpus, tagged A1-C1 in equal bands ([Språkbanken, Kelly](https://sprakbanken.se/en/resources/kelly)); the 2020 LREC paper says each level A1-C1 holds 1,404 entries ([Graën et al. 2020](https://aclanthology.org/2020.lrec-1.43.pdf)), so A1+A2+B1 comes to about 4,212 lemmas by my arithmetic. Because the bands are frequency ranks and not what B1 learners know, this is not a vocabulary target; no reliable source in the notes states the vocabulary size needed for B1 (**not found**). A vendor claims Duolingo's Swedish reaches about A2 with 2,000-2,500 words ([Clozemaster blog](https://www.clozemaster.com/blog/?p=7904), vendor). Plan for your passive vocabulary to grow far beyond 300 and let exam-specific themes drive which words come first.

**Study method.** Learn by theme (Appendix A), with the English translation on one side and a Swedish sentence on the other; test yourself actively (say the Swedish from the English cue) and use spaced repetition (Command 3). Learn each noun with *en/ett*. Learn verbs as the present form plus the past form (*gå, går, gick*).

## Part 11 — An honest roadmap from A0

### 11.1 What the evidence says about time

No source gives a measured, Swedish-specific A0-to-B1 time for adult learners ([notes: resources_feasibility, section b]). Two non-Swedish-YKI anchors exist. The US State Department lists Swedish in FSI Category I ("languages close to English") at **24 weeks, 552 class hours**, assuming a typical week of **23 class hours plus 17 hours of self-study**, with the target ILR 3 ("professional working proficiency"), a standard no source in the notes maps to CEFR (it is plausibly above B1, which is an unsupported inference) ([US State Department](https://www.state.gov/foreign-language-training/)). That is about 552 + 408 = 960 hours by my arithmetic. Cambridge English gives cumulative guided learning hours from beginner of A1 90-100, A2 180-200, **B1 350-400**, B2 500-600, "intended as a guideline only" and counts guided teaching, not self-study ([Cambridge English](https://support.cambridgeenglish.org/hc/en-gb/articles/202838506-Guided-learning-hours)). The **350-960 hour range is my inference** from these two anchors; it is not a measured timeline and not specific to Swedish YKI. A blogger who passed all four subtests at level 3 reports roughly 9-12 months, starting level unstated ([Chum Van](https://chumvan.github.io/post/swedish-ci), anecdotal). Vendor claims of 6-12 months at 5 hours a week (about 130-260 hours) or at two hours a day (about 360-730 hours) are marketing and the first falls below Cambridge's lower anchor ([Clozemaster](https://www.clozemaster.com/blog/?p=7904); [Online Swedish](https://onlineswedish.org/yki-preparation/)). Whether Hindi/Marathi helps or hinders was not researched; fluent English is likely to help vocabulary and sentence structure, because Swedish is Germanic, but no source tests this **[Inference]**.

### 11.5 Daily timetables

There are no evidence-based "optimal minute splits" for YKI; these are expert designs built on three principles: active retrieval (saying and writing, not rereading), spacing (daily short vocabulary), and feedback (record then check; compare with a model). They are **not equally divided across subtests**: the foundation phase puts most time on production patterns and vocabulary; the exam phase puts most time on timed tasks of your *target pair*. **Pair tilt:** if your target is writing + listening, shift 10-15% of speaking minutes to listening dictation and writing; if speaking + reading, shift writing minutes to speaking and reading.

**Foundation phase (weeks 1-12):**

| Block | 30 min | 60 min | 90 min | 120 min |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Spaced-repetition vocabulary (say aloud) | 10 | 15 | 15 | 20 |
| Grammar or pattern drill (produce 5-10 sentences) | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 |
| Input: listening or reading, alternate days, with questions | 10 | 15 | 25 | 30 |
| Speaking: record 1-2 minutes, play back | - | 10 | 15 | 25 |
| Writing: 5-10 sentences from a template | - | 5 | 10 | 15 |
| Error review and corrections | - | - | 5 | 5 |
| Day 7 | 30-min self-check | 45-min self-check | 60-min self-check | 60-min self-check plus checkpoint |

**Exam phase (week 13 to the sitting):**

| Block | 30 min | 60 min | 90 min | 120 min |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Phrases and vocabulary review | 5 | 10 | 10 | 15 |
| Timed task of the target pair (rotate S1-S3, writing task, listening or reading set) | 15 | 25 | 35 | 45 |
| Error correction against the checklist | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 |
| Input at normal speed (Yle clip, notice) | 5 | 10 | 15 | 20 |
| Second timed task or feedback session | - | 5 | 15 | 20 |

A realistic weekly rhythm: six study days plus one lighter day; every second Sunday a self-check; every fourth week a longer timed mock. A 30-minute day will not reach the 350-hour anchor in any window in the notes (84 hours in 24 weeks); it is only a maintenance level.

## Part 12 — Last-minute and emergency preparation

**What emergency preparation can and cannot do.** It cannot make an A0 or A1 candidate reliably reach level 3 in a week; the hours anchors above rule that out. It can reduce *avoidable* losses for a candidate already near the pass line: unfinished tasks, missed content points, misread instructions, unrehearsed formats and fatigue.

| | **7 days remaining** | **3 days remaining** | **24 hours remaining** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Highest-priority practice** | One timed task per day of each type: monologue, phone call, voicemail, one informal and one formal writing task | Re-do your two weakest tasks; record and review | One 30-minute light run-through of formats; no new material |
| **Essential phrases** | Phone greeting/closing, apology, reason (*eftersom*), request, opinion, three-reasons openers, letter openers/closers (Part 4, Appendix F-D) | The 10 phrases you personally use most | Read the 10 phrases once |
| **Task formats to revisit** | The seven verified sample tasks; reread the speaking time limits (10-25 s, 1 min + 1.5 min, 20 s) and writing content points | Same, plus the reading and listening question types | Same, no timing |
| **Time management** | Practise starting an answer within 3 seconds and ending when the turn ends | Rehearse the "move on after a miss" rule | Plan the day: arrival time, travel, meal |
| **Rules to check** | Registration confirmation, the site and start time (sent by e-mail shortly before), valid photo ID (not a driving licence), no electronic devices including watches, pencil provided, latecomers cannot sit ([OPH, before the test](https://www.oph.fi/sv/utbildning-och-examina/fore-yki-testdagen)) | Same | Same; set two alarms |
| **Things to avoid** | New grammar topics; long memorised answers; cramming vocabulary lists | Learning new grammar; late-night mock; comparing with others | Cramming; extra mock exams; caffeine late; alcohol; unfamiliar food |

**Do not**, at any point, look for leaked tasks or "recalled questions"; none are used here and doing so risks cheating consequences (loss of the right to take YKI in that language for 6 or 12 months, per OPH).

## Part 14 — Exam-day playbook

Everything here uses the official OPH instructions and the practice-site rules ([OPH, before the test](https://www.oph.fi/sv/utbildning-och-examina/fore-yki-testdagen); [JYU instructions](https://ykitesti2.solki.jyu.fi/ykitesti2/web/en/node/2)). Where a detail is not published for the digital test it is marked **not found**.

**The evening before.** Confirm the site and start time in the e-mail the site sends shortly before the day; pack valid photo ID (passport, ID card, residence permit card; not a driving licence); pack a packed lunch (OPH allows it) and water; plan travel with a buffer; sleep. Do a 20-minute light run-through of the formats and no new learning.

**Arrival and identification.** Arrive early: latecomers cannot take the test, and if you are late on a multi-part day you cannot take a part already started. At the start, phones, watches, laptops, tablets and other electronic devices are collected and not available even during breaks. Pencil and eraser are provided. For a digital test you use a test-site computer; typing rules, headset and keyboard details are **not found**.

**What not to bring.** Dictionaries, study materials, any electronic device including a smartwatch. OPH says cheating, ignoring supervisor instructions, taking test material and disrupting others lead to a fail result and to losing the right to take YKI in that language for 6 or 12 months. Do not communicate with other candidates.

**Understanding instructions.** Read every instruction line twice. Swedish tasks and instructions are in Swedish only, so the vocabulary in Part 4 and Appendix F-A matters. Answer in the language the task asks.

**Managing time.** The per-subtest time is **not found**. In speaking, each turn has a time given in parentheses; use the whole turn for relevant content but do not pad. In writing, plan your time proportionally (about 20% planning and content checklist, 60% writing, 20% checking) **[Coaching advice]**; complete all three tasks. In reading and listening answer every item; wrong answers are not penalised in the reading sample.

**Unfamiliar topics.** Use the ladder (Part 6C): say the simplest true thing; describe a missing word; keep to the required points. Remember that the OPH topic areas stay the same at all levels and tasks are based on everyday situations.

**Recovering from mistakes and deciding when to move on.** In speaking, correct yourself once (*Förlåt, jag menar...*) and go on; do not restart. In reading and listening, give a best guess and move on. In writing, cross out neatly and continue.

**Recorded speaking stress.** The other voice is recorded and you cannot negotiate; the recording is rated later by assessors who do not know you. Breathe, say your first sentence, and answer only what was asked. If noise from other candidates is a problem, a 2022 forum report suggests requesting special arrangements at registration, which must be done during the registration period with an expert statement ([OPH, registration](https://www.oph.fi/sv/utbildning-och-examina/anmalan-till-yki-test)).

**Checking writing.** One pass for content points (all ticked?); one for the top three recurring errors (verb second, *inte*, en/ett) in your own list; make sure the greeting and closing suit the register; write clearly by hand on paper tests ("Skriv med tydlig handstil"); for a digital test, typing rules are **not found**.

## Part 15 — Your personal exam coaching system

**How to use it.** Paste one command at a time into a new chat with ChatGPT (or any assistant). Each command is self-contained and carries the verified exam limits, so you do not need to paste anything else first. Four honest limits: (1) an AI is **not an official rater**, so every "level estimate" it gives is a practice estimate; (2) AI Swedish can contain mistakes, so check anything important with a teacher or a Swedish speaker; (3) a text chat cannot simulate the real recorded studio or computer, the official audio or the digital interface; (4) do not paste personal data such as your ID number, address or certificate. Examples in the appendices use invented names.

## Conclusion

Three findings change how this project should be run. First, the exam's *structure*, not Swedish itself, creates the shortest legal path: two spring-2027 subtest-only days (3 March for writing + listening, 19 May for speaking + reading) each cover a complete accepted pair for citizenship or permanent residence, at 113 and 127 EUR by OPH's own subtest prices, which is why the plan targets one pair rather than four equal subtests. That convenience rests on an unresolved conflict between OPH's same-day rule and Migri's allowance for combined certificates, so the first action is a question to Migri, not a vocabulary list. Second, the evidence base for *how to study* is far thinner than the confident tone of most exam-prep advice implies: the listening and reading sample items could not be read, per-subtest times and digital-test mechanics are unpublished, there is no official guidance on memorised answers, and five candidate accounts exist, none of which supports a claim of what is typical. The recommendations here therefore lean on what is solid (the criteria wording, the verified production task types, the content checklists printed in the sample tasks) and label everything else as an expert estimate.

Third, the honest arithmetic is unforgiving for an A0 start. The two available anchors imply 350 to 960 total hours, and no 4-to-24-week schedule in the notes delivers even 350 hours unless you study two hours every day for about 25 weeks. The most useful way to treat spring 2027 is therefore as a decision calendar (14 Jan, 15 Feb, 1 Apr and 3 May registration deadlines) against which two-weekly checkpoints decide whether to register, while the Arbis entrance test on 15 December 2026 is the one concrete, dated step for building the base. Level 3 is a *communicate-and-be-understood* standard, not a perfection standard, so the winning trade is to spend hours on correct basic sentences, task checklists, numbers and timed speaking, and to defer almost everything else. The remaining uncertainty, above all how the digital Swedish test is delivered and timed, should be resolved by reading OPH's practice tests and test-day instructions again before you book.

## Appendix B — 30 speaking prompts

**How to use.** Time yourself. **Monologue prompts (B1-B8):** 1 minute of preparation with paper notes, then up to 1.5 minutes (verified for the *Berätta* sample; the topics in B2-B8 are inferred). **Phone-turn prompts (B9-B18):** the other speaker's line is given; answer within the time shown (the samples use 10-25 s turns). **Voicemail prompts (B19-B24):** 20 seconds. **Opinion prompts (B25-B30):** about one minute (inferred format; the official samples do not show a spoken opinion task, but the 2011 basis lists "giving opinions" among speaking task types). Sample answers contain invented personal details; replace them with your own true details. Each prompt has: Swedish question, English meaning, outline, sample answer with translation, reusable expressions, likely beginner errors, and a harder follow-up. All are **original practice material, not official**. Type tag **V** or **I** as in the Appendices note.

### Monologues

**B1 (V, monologue).** *Berätta om en person i din familj som är viktig för dig.* (Tell about a person in your family who is important to you.)
- *Outline:* who → why important → what like → how long known → best thing → what we do together → what I want to say.
- *Sample:* "Jag vill berätta om min mamma. Hon är den viktigaste personen i mitt liv. Hon är snäll, stark och mycket hjälpsam. När jag var liten arbetade hon hårt för att vi skulle ha mat och kläder. Det bästa med henne är att hon aldrig ger upp. Nu bor hon långt härifrån, men vi pratar i telefon varje dag. Jag vill säga till henne att jag är mycket tacksam." (I want to tell about my mother. She is the most important person in my life. She is kind, strong and very helpful. When I was little she worked hard so that we would have food and clothes. The best thing about her is that she never gives up. Now she lives far from here, but we talk on the phone every day. I want to tell her that I am very grateful.)
- *Expressions:* Jag vill berätta om... / Det bästa med... är att... / Vi pratar... varje...
- *Likely errors:* *Min mamma hon är snäll* (do not repeat the subject); *Jag har känner henne*; forgetting *att* after *Det bästa med henne är*.
- *Harder follow-up:* Berätta också hur er relation har förändrats över tid. (Also tell how your relationship has changed over time.)

**B2 (I, monologue).** *Berätta om en plats som är viktig för dig, till exempel din hemstad.* (Tell about a place that is important to you, for example your home town.)
- *Outline:* where → what it looks like → what you do there → why important → wish.
- *Sample:* "Jag vill berätta om min hemstad. Den ligger vid en flod och är ganska stor. Där finns många parker och en stor marknad. Jag växte upp där, och mina föräldrar bor kvar. Jag gick i skolan där och hade många vänner. Den är viktig för mig eftersom jag har så många minnen därifrån. Jag skulle gärna besöka den oftare." (I want to tell about my home town. It lies by a river and is fairly big. There are many parks and a big market. I grew up there and my parents still live there. I went to school there and had many friends. It is important to me because I have so many memories from there. I would like to visit it more often.)
- *Expressions:* Den ligger... / Där finns... / Jag växte upp... / Jag skulle gärna...
- *Likely errors:* mixing *i* and *på* with place names; forgetting that *bor kvar* means "still live there".
- *Harder:* Jämför din hemstad med staden där du bor nu. (Compare your home town with the town where you live now.)

**B3 (V-like, monologue).** *Berätta om en person som du beundrar. Vem är det, och varför?* (Tell about a person you admire. Who is it, and why?)
- *Outline:* who → what they do/did → two qualities → one example → what I learned.
- *Sample:* "Jag beundrar min lärare från skolan, herr Joshi. Han var sträng men rättvis. Han förklarade svåra saker på ett enkelt sätt. Jag lärde mig mycket av honom, inte bara matematik utan också hur man arbetar hårt. Han tog sig tid för alla elever. Jag tänker ofta på honom när jag själv har svåra uppgifter." (I admire my teacher from school, Mr Joshi. He was strict but fair. He explained difficult things in a simple way. I learned a lot from him, not only mathematics but also how to work hard. He took time for all pupils. I often think of him when I myself have difficult tasks.)
- *Expressions:* Jag beundrar... / ...men... / inte bara... utan också... / Jag lärde mig...
- *Errors:* *Jag lär mig* (present) for the past (use *lärde mig*); *inte bara... men också* (use *inte bara... utan också*).
- *Harder:* Vad skulle du vilja säga till honom i dag? (What would you like to say to him today?)

**B4 (I, monologue).** *Berätta om en dag som du aldrig glömmer.* (Tell about a day you will never forget.)
- *Outline:* which day → where → what happened (först, sedan) → feelings → why unforgettable.
- *Sample:* "En dag som jag aldrig glömmer är dagen då jag kom till Finland. Det var i november och redan mörkt klockan fyra. Jag var trött efter den långa resan, men också mycket glad. En vän mötte mig på flygplatsen, och vi åkte till hans lägenhet. Där åt vi varm soppa. Jag tänkte: nu börjar mitt nya liv." (A day I will never forget is the day I came to Finland. It was in November and already dark at four. I was tired after the long trip, but also very happy. A friend met me at the airport and we went to his flat. There we ate hot soup. I thought: now my new life begins.)
- *Expressions:* En dag som jag aldrig glömmer är... / Det var... / Jag var trött men glad.
- *Errors:* tense mixing (*Jag kommer* for *kom*); *en gång* vs *dagen då*.
- *Harder:* Förklara hur din känsla har ändrats sedan dess. (Explain how your feeling has changed since then.)

**B5 (I, monologue).** *Berätta om ditt drömjobb.* (Tell about your dream job.)
- *Outline:* the job → why → what I need first → plan.
- *Sample:* "Mitt drömjobb är att vara lärare. Jag tycker om att förklara saker för andra. Jag skulle vilja undervisa barn i matematik. Det är ett viktigt jobb eftersom barn behöver bra lärare. Först måste jag lära mig bättre svenska. Sedan vill jag studera på universitetet. Jag hoppas att jag kan börja arbeta om några år." (My dream job is to be a teacher. I like to explain things to others. I would like to teach children mathematics. It is an important job because children need good teachers. First I must learn better Swedish. Then I want to study at the university. I hope I can start working in a few years.)
- *Expressions:* Mitt drömjobb är... / Jag skulle vilja... / Först måste jag... sedan...
- *Errors:* *Jag vill att bli*; no *att* after *vill*: *Jag vill bli lärare*.
- *Harder:* Vilka svårigheter kan du möta, och hur löser du dem? (What difficulties could you face and how do you solve them?)

**B6 (I, monologue).** *Berätta om din bästa semester.* (Tell about your best holiday.)
- *Outline:* when and where → who with → what you did → weather → why best.
- *Sample:* "Min bästa semester var för tre år sedan. Vi åkte till havet med hela familjen. Vi bodde på ett litet hotell nära stranden. Varje dag badade vi och åt god fisk. Vädret var varmt och soligt. Barnen hade jättekul. Jag skulle gärna åka dit igen." (My best holiday was three years ago. We went to the sea with the whole family. We stayed at a small hotel near the beach. Every day we swam and ate good fish. The weather was warm and sunny. The children had great fun. I would gladly go there again.)
- *Expressions:* för tre år sedan / Vi åkte till... / Vi bodde... / Varje dag...
- *Errors:* *Vi bodde i hotell* (use *på ett hotell*); *för tre år sedan* is the written form (*sen* is spoken).
- *Harder:* Jämför den med en dålig resa du har gjort. (Compare it with a bad trip you have made.)

**B7 (I, monologue).** *Berätta om din familj.* (Tell about your family.)
- *Outline:* who is in it → ages/jobs → where they live → what we do together.
- *Sample:* "Jag har en liten familj. Jag är gift och vi har en dotter som är fem år. Min fru arbetar på ett sjukhus, och jag arbetar som ingenjör. Våra föräldrar bor långt borta, så vi besöker dem en gång om året. På helgerna går vi ofta till lekparken. Vi gillar att vara tillsammans." (I have a small family. I am married and we have a daughter who is five years old. My wife works at a hospital and I work as an engineer. Our parents live far away, so we visit them once a year. At weekends we often go to the playground. We like being together.)
- *Expressions:* Jag har... / Vi är... / på helgerna / en gång om året
- *Errors:* learners say *har fem år* (copying other languages); use *är fem år*; mixing *en/ett* (*en dotter, ett barn*).
- *Harder:* Hur ser din familj ut om tio år? (What does your family look like in ten years?)

**B8 (I, monologue).** *Berätta om ett problem som du hade och hur du löste det.* (Tell about a problem you had and how you solved it.)
- *Outline:* the problem → what you tried first → what you did then → result → what you learned.
- *Sample:* "Förra månaden hade jag ett problem med internet. Det fungerade inte i tre dagar. Först startade jag om routern, men det hjälpte inte. Sedan ringde jag till företaget. De skickade en tekniker på fredagen. Han bytte en kabel, och nu fungerar allt. Jag lärde mig att man ska ringa direkt när något är fel." (Last month I had a problem with the internet. It did not work for three days. First I restarted the router, but it did not help. Then I called the company. They sent a technician on Friday. He changed a cable and now everything works. I learned that you should call right away when something is wrong.)
- *Expressions:* Jag hade ett problem med... / Först... men det hjälpte inte. / Nu fungerar allt.
- *Errors:* verb tense; *Det hjälper inte* for *Det hjälpte inte*.
- *Harder:* Vad skulle du göra annorlunda nästa gång? (What would you do differently next time?)

### Phone turns (the other speaker's line in italics)

**B9 (V, 20 s).** *Röst: "Hej! Jag ringer angående cykeln i annonsen. Är den fortfarande till salu? Hur gammal är den?"* (Voice: "Hi! I'm calling about the bike in the ad. Is it still for sale? How old is it?")
- *Outline:* yes → age → condition → price.
- *Sample:* "Hej! Ja, cykeln är fortfarande till salu. Den är tre år gammal och i bra skick. Jag har alltid ställt den inomhus. Priset är 120 euro." (Hi! Yes, the bike is still for sale. It is three years old and in good condition. I have always kept it indoors. The price is 120 euros.)
- *Expressions:* Ja, den är fortfarande till salu. / Den är ... år gammal. / Den är i bra skick.
- *Errors:* *Den är tre år* (add *gammal*); using *det* for *cykeln*.
- *Harder:* Add that it has a small scratch and the price can be discussed: "Den har en liten repa. Priset kan diskuteras."

**B10 (V, 20 s).** *Röst: "Kan jag komma och titta på den? När passar det för dig?"* (Can I come and look at it? When suits you?)
- *Outline:* yes → two time options → ask which suits.
- *Sample:* "Ja, gärna. Det passar bra i morgon efter klockan fem. Eller på lördag förmiddag. Vilken tid passar dig bäst?" (Yes, gladly. Tomorrow after five suits well. Or on Saturday morning. Which time suits you best?)
- *Expressions:* Det passar bra... / Vilken tid passar dig?
- *Errors:* *Jag passar i morgon* (the time suits you, not you: *Det passar i morgon*); *Det passar mig inte* is correct for "it does not suit me".
- *Harder:* Say you can also come to him: "Jag kan också komma till dig."

**B11 (V, 25 s).** *Röst: "Var bor du? Hur kommer jag dit?"* (Where do you live? How do I get there?)
- *Outline:* address → landmark → how to travel → description.
- *Sample:* "Jag bor på Skolgatan 3 i Vanda. Det är nära stationen. Du kan ta tåget till Dickursby och sedan gå fem minuter. Det är ett gult hus med röd dörr." (I live at Skolgatan 3 in Vantaa. It is near the station. You can take the train to Dickursby and then walk five minutes. It is a yellow house with a red door.)
- *Expressions:* Jag bor på... / Det är nära... / Du kan ta... och sedan gå...
- *Errors:* *på/i* with towns; after *kan* use the infinitive: *Du kan ta tåget och sedan gå*, not *Du kan tar*.
- *Harder:* Give directions from a bus stop: "Gå rakt fram och sväng till höger vid kiosken."

**B12 (V, 25 s).** *Röst: "Fixa Rätt, goddag. Vad kan jag hjälpa till med?"* (Fix-Right, good day. How can I help?)
- *Outline:* greet and name → what is wrong → what you did → what you want.
- *Sample:* "Hej, jag heter [namn]. Min tv fungerar inte. Bilden är borta. Jag lämnade in den för två veckor sedan, men ingen har ringt mig. Jag vill veta när den blir klar." (Hi, my name is [name]. My TV does not work. The picture is gone. I left it for repair two weeks ago, but nobody has called me. I want to know when it will be ready.)
- *Expressions:* Hej, jag heter... / ...fungerar inte. / ...för två veckor sedan.
- *Errors:* *Min tv gör inte fungerar*; *sedan två veckor*.
- *Harder:* Also say you called before: "Jag har ringt två gånger tidigare."

**B13 (V, 20 s).** *Röst: "Vi har lagat den, tror jag. Vad är problemet nu?"* (We have repaired it, I think. What is the problem now?)
- *Outline:* protest → what still doesn't work → what you tried → disappointment.
- *Sample:* "Nej, den är inte lagad. Bilden är tillbaka, men ljudet fungerar inte. Jag har provat olika kanaler, men det är samma sak. Jag är besviken, eftersom ni lovade att allt skulle vara klart förra veckan." (No, it is not repaired. The picture is back but the sound does not work. I have tried different channels but it is the same. I am disappointed because you promised everything would be ready last week.)
- *Expressions:* Nej, den är inte lagad. / Jag är besviken eftersom... / ni lovade...
- *Errors:* *Den inte är lagad* (in a main clause *inte* comes after the verb: *Den är inte lagad*).
- *Harder:* Ask for compensation: "Kan jag få rabatt för besväret?"

**B14 (V, 15 s).** *Röst: "Vi kan komma på torsdag mellan åtta och sexton. Passar det?"* (We can come on Thursday between 8 and 16. Does that suit?)
- *Outline:* say no politely with reason → propose another time.
- *Sample:* "Torsdag passar tyvärr inte, eftersom jag arbetar. Kan ni komma på fredag efter klockan fyra? Annars kan jag vara hemma på lördag." (Thursday unfortunately does not suit because I am working. Can you come on Friday after four? Otherwise I can be at home on Saturday.)
- *Expressions:* ...passar tyvärr inte, eftersom... / Annars kan jag...
- *Errors:* *Det passar inte till mig*.
- *Harder:* Also ask them to phone first: "Ring gärna en timme innan."

**B15 (I, 20 s).** *Röst (mottagning): "Vilken tid passar dig? Vi har lediga tider på tisdag klockan nio och torsdag klockan tretton."* (Which time suits you? We have free times on Tuesday at 9 and Thursday at 13.)
- *Sample:* "Tisdag klockan nio passar mig bra. Mitt namn är [namn], och mitt telefonnummer är noll fyra noll, ett två tre, fyra fem sex sju. Tack för hjälpen!" (Tuesday at nine suits me well. My name is [name] and my phone number is 040 123 4567. Thanks for the help!)
- *Outline:* choose → name → number → thanks.
- *Expressions:* ...passar mig bra. / Mitt telefonnummer är...
- *Errors:* number reading; *tretton* vs *trettio*.
- *Harder:* Ask if you can bring a relative.

**B16 (I, 20 s).** *Röst (restaurang): "Hur många är ni, och vilken dag vill ni komma?"* (How many are you and which day do you want to come?)
- *Sample:* "Vi är fyra personer. Vi vill komma på lördag klockan sju. Kan vi få ett bord vid fönstret? En av oss äter inte kött." (We are four people. We want to come on Saturday at seven. Can we have a table by the window? One of us does not eat meat.)
- *Outline:* how many → when → request → extra information.
- *Expressions:* Vi är... personer. / Kan vi få...?
- *Errors:* *Vi äter inte kött en av oss* (put the subject first: *En av oss äter inte kött*).
- *Harder:* Ask about the menu and prices.

**B17 (I, 25 s).** *Röst (hyresvärd): "Jag fick ditt meddelande om hissen. Vad är problemet exakt?"* (I received your message about the lift. What exactly is the problem?)
- *Sample:* "Hissen fungerar inte sedan i måndags. Många äldre grannar måste gå i trappor. Jag tycker att ni ska laga den så snart som möjligt." (The lift has not worked since Monday. Many elderly neighbours must use the stairs. I think you should repair it as soon as possible.)
- *Outline:* problem → since when → effect → request.
- *Expressions:* ...fungerar inte sedan... / så snart som möjligt.
- *Errors:* *sedan måndag* means "since Monday" but *för två dagar sedan* means "two days ago"; do not mix them; *Hissen har inte fungerar* (use *fungerar*).
- *Harder:* Say what you will do if nothing happens.

**B18 (I, 25 s).** *Röst (arbetsgivare): "Vi måste ändra ditt arbetspass i morgon. Kan du börja klockan sju i stället för nio?"* (We have to change your shift tomorrow. Can you start at seven instead of nine?)
- *Sample:* "Det passar tyvärr inte, eftersom jag måste lämna barnet på daghemmet klockan åtta. Jag kan börja klockan tio i stället, eller arbeta längre på kvällen. Går det bra?" (That unfortunately does not suit because I must leave the child at the day-care centre at eight. I can start at ten instead, or work longer in the evening. Is that OK?)
- *Outline:* say no → reason → offer alternatives → ask.
- *Expressions:* Det passar tyvärr inte... / eller... / Går det bra?
- *Errors:* *Jag kan att börja* (no *att* after *kan*).
- *Harder:* Ask for the change to be written down.

### Voicemails (20 s)

**B19 (V-type).** *Du kan inte komma till Elins fest i kväll. Lämna ett meddelande till henne.* (You cannot come to Elin's party tonight. Leave her a message.)
- *Sample:* "Hej Elin, det är [namn]. Tyvärr kan jag inte komma till festen i kväll, eftersom jag är sjuk. Jag är mycket ledsen. Jag hoppas att ni får en trevlig kväll. Vi ses snart!" (Hi Elin, it's [name]. Unfortunately I cannot come to the party tonight because I am ill. I am very sorry. I hope you have a nice evening. See you soon!)
- *Outline:* greet → can't come → reason → apology → good wishes.
- *Expressions:* Tyvärr kan jag inte... / Jag är mycket ledsen.
- *Errors:* *Jag kan inte att komma* (no *att* after *kan*).
- *Harder:* Offer to meet next week.

**B20 (V-type).** *Du har glömt plånboken hemma och blir sen. Lämna ett meddelande till din vän.* (You have forgotten your wallet at home and will be late. Leave your friend a message.)
- *Sample:* "Hej Maria, det är [namn]. Jag har glömt min plånbok hemma, så jag måste åka tillbaka. Jag blir ungefär en halvtimme sen. Kan du köpa biljetterna? Jag betalar dig sedan. Tack!" (Hi Maria, it's [name]. I have forgotten my wallet at home, so I have to go back. I will be about half an hour late. Can you buy the tickets? I will pay you afterwards. Thanks!)
- *Expressions:* Jag har glömt... / Kan du...? / Jag betalar dig sedan.
- *Errors:* *Jag har glömde* (use *har glömt*).
- *Harder:* Suggest a new meeting place.

**B21 (V-type).** *Du vill flytta ett möte från torsdag till fredag. Lämna ett meddelande.* (You want to move a meeting from Thursday to Friday. Leave a message.)
- *Sample:* "Hej Anna, det är [namn]. Jag måste tyvärr flytta vårt möte på torsdag. Kan vi ses på fredag klockan tio i stället? Ring mig gärna eller skriv ett meddelande. Tack!" (Hi Anna, it's [name]. I unfortunately have to move our meeting on Thursday. Can we meet on Friday at ten instead? Please call me or write a message. Thanks!)
- *Expressions:* Jag måste tyvärr flytta... / i stället / Ring mig gärna.
- *Errors:* *flytta* means move to another time; *byta* means swap.
- *Harder:* Give a reason.

**B22 (V-type).** *Du hittade en jacka som tillhör din vän. Lämna ett meddelande.* (You found a jacket that belongs to your friend. Leave a message.)
- *Sample:* "Hej Johan, det är [namn]. Jag hittade din svarta jacka i går efter festen. Den hänger hemma hos mig. Du kan hämta den när du vill. Ring mig så bestämmer vi en tid." (Hi Johan, it's [name]. I found your black jacket yesterday after the party. It is hanging at my place. You can collect it whenever you like. Call me and we will fix a time.)
- *Expressions:* Jag hittade... / Du kan hämta den... / Ring mig så bestämmer vi...
- *Errors:* *hos mig* vs *i mig*.
- *Harder:* Offer to bring it to him.

**B23 (V-type).** *Du ringer till tandläkaren och måste avboka din tid.* (You call the dentist and must cancel your appointment.)
- *Sample:* "Hej, det här är [namn]. Jag har en tid hos tandläkaren på onsdag klockan nio. Tyvärr måste jag avboka den, eftersom jag är sjuk. Kan jag få en ny tid nästa vecka? Mitt nummer är noll fyra noll, ett två tre, fyra fem sex sju. Tack!" (Hi, this is [name]. I have an appointment with the dentist on Wednesday at nine. Unfortunately I have to cancel it because I am ill. Can I get a new time next week? My number is 040 123 4567. Thanks!)
- *Expressions:* Jag har en tid... / avboka / Kan jag få en ny tid...?
- *Errors:* *Jag måste avbokar* (after *måste* use the infinitive *avboka*).
- *Harder:* Ask about a cancellation fee.

**B24 (V-type).** *Du kommer sent till en middag med en kollega eftersom du är kvar på jobbet. Lämna ett meddelande.* (You will be late to a dinner with a colleague because you are still at work. Leave a message.)
- *Sample:* "Hej Lars, det är [namn]. Jag är tyvärr kvar på jobbet, eftersom ett möte tog längre tid. Jag kommer kvart över sju i stället för sju. Börja gärna äta utan mig. Förlåt!" (Hi Lars, it's [name]. I am unfortunately still at work because a meeting took longer. I will come at quarter past seven instead of seven. Please start eating without me. Sorry!)
- *Expressions:* Jag är kvar på... / i stället för / Börja gärna utan mig.
- *Errors:* *kvart över* vs *kvart i*.
- *Harder:* Suggest meeting at the restaurant door.

### Opinions (about one minute; inferred format)

**B25 (I).** *Är det bättre att bo i en stad eller på landet? Ge din åsikt och skäl.* (Is it better to live in a city or in the countryside? Give your opinion and reasons.)
- *Outline:* opinion → reason 1 → reason 2 → example → counterpoint → conclusion.
- *Sample:* "Jag tycker att det är bättre att bo i en stad. För det första finns det fler jobb. För det andra är det lätt att åka buss eller tåg. Till exempel kan jag åka till jobbet utan bil. Visserligen är det dyrare, men jag tycker att det är värt det. Därför vill jag bo kvar i stan." (I think it is better to live in a city. First, there are more jobs. Second, it is easy to take a bus or train. For example I can go to work without a car. It is admittedly more expensive, but I think it is worth it. Therefore I want to keep living in the city.)
- *Expressions:* Jag tycker att... / För det första... / Visserligen..., men...
- *Errors:* *mer dyrt* (use *dyrare*); *i staden/stan*.
- *Harder:* Say what would make you move to the countryside.

**B26 (I).** *Är det bra att arbeta hemifrån? Ge din åsikt.* (Is it good to work from home? Give your opinion.)
- *Sample:* "Jag tycker att det är bra att arbeta hemifrån ibland. Man sparar tid eftersom man inte behöver resa. Man kan också arbeta lugnare. Men det är lätt att bli ensam. Därför tycker jag att man ska arbeta hemma två dagar i veckan och på kontoret resten." (I think it is good to work from home sometimes. You save time because you do not need to travel. You can also work more calmly. But it is easy to become lonely. Therefore I think one should work at home two days a week and at the office the rest.)
- *Outline:* opinion → advantage → advantage → disadvantage → compromise.
- *Expressions:* Man sparar tid eftersom... / Men... / Därför tycker jag att...
- *Errors:* *hemma* (being at home) versus *hem* (going home); in an *eftersom* clause *inte* goes before the verb: *eftersom man inte behöver resa*.
- *Harder:* Compare with your own job.

**B27 (I; theme from the verified writing sample).** *Tycker du att dagens mode är snyggt?* (Do you think today's fashion is nice?)
- *Sample:* "Nej, jag tycker inte att dagens mode är så snyggt. Många kläder är för stora och har konstiga färger. Dessutom är kläderna ofta dyra. Jag föredrar enkla kläder i fina färger. Men jag förstår att unga människor gillar det." (No, I do not think today's fashion is so nice. Many clothes are too big and have strange colours. In addition the clothes are often expensive. I prefer simple clothes in nice colours. But I understand that young people like it.)
- *Outline:* opinion → reason → reason → preference → fair point.
- *Expressions:* Jag tycker inte att... / Dessutom... / Jag föredrar...
- *Errors:* *Jag tycker att inte dagens mode är snyggt* (use *Jag tycker inte att...*); *mode* is an ett-word, so *snyggt*.
- *Harder:* Describe what you wear to work and why.

**B28 (I; theme from the verified writing sample).** *Ska skolan lära barn om pengar?* (Should school teach children about money?)
- *Sample:* "Ja, det tycker jag. Många barn vet inte vad saker kostar. I skolan kan de lära sig att spara och att välja. Det är bra för framtiden. Därför ska skolan ha lektioner om pengar." (Yes, I think so. Many children do not know what things cost. At school they can learn to save and to choose. That is good for the future. Therefore school should have lessons about money.)
- *Outline:* opinion → reason 1 → reason 2 → conclusion.
- *Expressions:* Ja, det tycker jag. / Därför ska...
- *Errors:* *Jag tycker ja* (say *Ja, det tycker jag*).
- *Harder:* What should parents teach at home?

**B29 (I).** *Vad tycker du om kollektivtrafiken i din stad?* (What do you think of public transport in your town?)
- *Sample:* "Jag tycker att kollektivtrafiken är ganska bra. Bussarna går ofta och biljetterna är inte för dyra. Ibland är bussarna försenade när det är snö. Jag önskar att det fanns fler bussar på natten. Överlag är jag nöjd." (I think public transport is quite good. The buses run often and the tickets are not too expensive. Sometimes the buses are delayed when there is snow. I wish there were more buses at night. Overall I am satisfied.)
- *Outline:* overall → good points → problem → wish → conclusion.
- *Expressions:* Jag önskar att det fanns... / Överlag...
- *Errors:* *Jag önskar att det finns* (use *fanns*); *Ibland bussarna är försenade* (verb second: *Ibland är bussarna försenade*).
- *Harder:* Suggest one improvement and say who should do it.

**B30 (I).** *Ska man försöka använda mindre plast? Ge din åsikt.* (Should one try to use less plastic? Give your opinion.)
- *Sample:* "Ja, jag tycker att man ska använda mindre plast. Plast är dåligt för naturen och djuren. Jag tar alltid med en egen kasse till affären. Det är lätt att göra, och det kostar ingenting. Om alla gör det blir det stor skillnad." (Yes, I think one should use less plastic. Plastic is bad for nature and animals. I always bring my own bag to the shop. It is easy to do and costs nothing. If everyone does it, it makes a big difference.)
- *Outline:* opinion → reason → personal example → easy → conclusion.
- *Expressions:* Om alla... blir det... / Jag tar alltid med...
- *Errors:* *om alla gör det, det blir* (omit the repeated subject).
- *Harder:* Respond to someone who says it does not matter.

## Appendix C — 20 writing prompts

**Common self-check (derived from the YKI writing criteria and the sample tasks; apply to every prompt).** Level 3 writing means simple but cohesive text on common topics, where unclear expressions sometimes disrupt reading. Check: (1) *Content:* every required point answered, with one clear sentence each; (2) *Register:* friendly for a friend, formal for a company or newspaper; (3) *Coherence:* ideas joined with *och, men, eftersom, så, för det första...*; (4) *Vocabulary:* simple, correct words; (5) *Accuracy:* verb in second place, *inte* after the verb, en/ett on the key nouns; (6) *Opening and closing* suited to the reader; (7) *Readable* handwriting on paper. Each prompt below adds its own tick-list. There is no official word count, so these models are short and complete (about 50-90 words). Type tags: **V** = modelled on a verified task type; **I** = inferred. All are **original practice material, not official**.

### Informal messages (verified type: e-mail to a friend)

**C1 (V).** *Skriv ett mejl till en vän. Du vet om en utställning på biblioteket som han eller hon skulle gilla. Berätta vad det är, när och var, och lägg till två egna detaljer. Öppna och avsluta meddelandet på ett lämpligt sätt.* (Write an e-mail to a friend. You know of an exhibition at the library that he or she would like. Say what it is, when and where, and add two details of your own. Open and close the message appropriately.)
- *Points:* what; when; where; two extra details; opening and closing.
- *Model:* "Hej Sara! Jag har hört om en fotoutställning på biblioteket i Esbo. Den öppnar på måndag den 8 mars och pågår hela veckan. Det är gratis att komma, och det finns också ett kafé i huset. Jag vet att du gillar foto, så jag tänkte tipsa dig. Vill du gå dit med mig på tisdag efter jobbet? Hälsningar, Rahul" (Hi Sara! I have heard about a photo exhibition at the library in Espoo. It opens on Monday 8 March and runs all week. It is free to come, and there is also a café in the building. I know you like photography, so I thought I would give you a tip. Do you want to go there with me on Tuesday after work? Regards, Rahul)
- *Reusable:* Jag har hört om... / Jag vet att du gillar..., så jag tänkte... / Vill du gå dit med mig...?
- *Common mistakes:* leaving out *where*; a list of facts without sentences; *Jag tänker du gillar* (use *Jag tror att du gillar*).
- *Tick-list:* ☐ what ☐ when ☐ where ☐ two extra details ☐ invitation ☐ greeting and closing.

**C2 (V).** *Din vän älskar mat. Skriv ett mejl om en matfestival i din stad. Berätta vad, när och var, och ge två extra detaljer.* (Your friend loves food. Write an e-mail about a food festival in your city. Say what, when and where, and give two extra details.)
- *Points:* what; when; where; two details.
- *Model:* "Hej Johan! Nu är det matfestival i Helsingfors. Den är på lördag och söndag klockan 11-18 vid Brunnsparken. Man kan smaka mat från tjugo länder, och musiken börjar klockan fyra. Jag vet att du älskar mat. Kan vi gå dit tillsammans på lördag? Skriv snart! Kram, Rahul" (Hi Johan! Now there is a food festival in Helsinki. It is on Saturday and Sunday from 11 to 18 at Kaivopuisto (Brunnsparken). You can taste food from twenty countries, and the music starts at four. I know you love food. Can we go there together on Saturday? Write soon! Hugs, Rahul)
- *Reusable:* Nu är det... / Man kan... / Kan vi gå dit tillsammans...?
- *Mistakes:* *Man kan smakar*; forgetting the times; no question.
- *Tick-list:* ☐ what ☐ days and times ☐ place ☐ two details ☐ question ☐ closing.

**C3 (I).** *Du ska ha middag hemma på lördag. Skriv en inbjudan till en kollega: när, var, vad ni ska äta, och be om svar.* (You are having dinner at home on Saturday. Write an invitation to a colleague: when, where, what you will eat, and ask for a reply.)
- *Points:* when; where; food; reply deadline.
- *Model:* "Hej Eva! Jag har middag hemma hos mig på lördag klockan 18. Jag bor på Parkvägen 12 i Vanda, våning 3. Jag lagar soppa och bakar bröd, och efter maten kan vi spela spel. Kan du komma? Svara gärna före torsdag. Hälsningar, Rahul" (Hi Eva! I am having dinner at my place on Saturday at 18:00. I live at Parkvägen 12 in Vantaa, floor 3. I am making soup and baking bread, and after the meal we can play games. Can you come? Please reply before Thursday. Regards, Rahul)
- *Reusable:* Jag har middag... / Jag bor på... / Svara gärna före...
- *Mistakes:* *Jag lagar soppa och bakar bröd och efter maten vi kan spela* (verb second after *efter maten kan vi*); missing address.
- *Tick-list:* ☐ time ☐ address ☐ food ☐ activity ☐ reply request ☐ closing.

**C4 (I).** *Du ska flytta på lördag. Skriv till en vän och be om hjälp. Berätta när, vad du behöver och vad du erbjuder.* (You are moving on Saturday. Write to a friend and ask for help. Say when, what you need and what you offer.)
- *Points:* when; what you need; offer.
- *Model:* "Hej Karim! Jag ska flytta på lördag, men jag har ingen bil. Kan du hjälpa mig med lådorna mellan klockan 10 och 13? Jag bjuder på lunch efteråt. Det är tre våningar utan hiss, tyvärr. Säg till om du kan! Tack på förhand! Kram, Rahul" (Hi Karim! I am moving on Saturday but I have no car. Can you help me with the boxes between 10 and 13? I will treat you to lunch afterwards. It is three floors without a lift, unfortunately. Let me know if you can! Thanks in advance! Hugs, Rahul)
- *Reusable:* Kan du hjälpa mig med...? / Jag bjuder på... / Säg till om du kan!
- *Mistakes:* *Kan du hjälper*; no time given.
- *Tick-list:* ☐ when ☐ need ☐ offer ☐ polite request ☐ thanks.

**C5 (I).** *Du missade ett möte med en vän i går. Be om ursäkt, förklara och föreslå en ny tid.* (You missed a meeting with a friend yesterday. Apologise, explain and suggest a new time.)
- *Points:* apology; reason; new time.
- *Model:* "Hej Lena! Förlåt att jag inte kom till kaféet i går. Mitt batteri tog slut, och jag glömde tiden. Jag skäms mycket. Kan vi träffas på torsdag i stället? Jag bjuder på kaffe och bulle som ursäkt. Hälsningar, Rahul" (Hi Lena! Sorry that I did not come to the café yesterday. My battery ran out and I forgot the time. I am very ashamed. Can we meet on Thursday instead? I will treat you to coffee and a bun as an apology. Regards, Rahul)
- *Reusable:* Förlåt att jag inte... / Kan vi träffas... i stället?
- *Mistakes:* *Förlåt att jag inte kom inte*; using present for yesterday.
- *Tick-list:* ☐ apology ☐ reason ☐ new time ☐ offer ☐ closing.

**C6 (I).** *Berätta för en vän om en resa du har gjort. Berätta vart du åkte, vad du gjorde och vad som var bäst.* (Tell a friend about a trip you made. Say where you went, what you did and what was best.)
- *Points:* where; what; best part.
- *Model:* "Hej Pia! Förra veckan åkte jag till Åbo med tåg. Jag gick runt i gamla stan och besökte slottet. Jag åt också en god lunch vid ån. Hugs, Rahul)

- *Mistakes:* *Förra veckan jag åkte* (verb second); *med tåget* versus *med tåg* (both fine).
- *Tick-list:* ☐ where ☐ what you did ☐ best part ☐ past tense ☐ closing.

### Opinion letters (verified type: letter to the editor, opinion and at least three reasons)

**C7 (V theme).** *Skriv en insändare: "Det är viktigt att lära barn pengars värde." Säg vad du skriver om, vad du tycker, och ge minst tre skäl.* (Write a letter to the editor: "It is important to teach children the value of money." Say what you are writing about, what you think, and give at least three reasons.)
- *Points:* topic; opinion; three reasons; conclusion.
- *Model:* "Hej! Jag skriver om pengar och barn. Jag tycker att man bör lära barn pengars värde tidigt, av tre skäl. För det första förstår barn då att pengar inte växer på träd. För det andra lär de sig att planera, till exempel att spara till en cykel. För det tredje får de bättre vanor som vuxna och tar färre lån. Därför bör både hem och skola prata om pengar. Med vänlig hälsning, [namn]" (Hello! I am writing about money and children. I think one should teach children the value of money early, for three reasons. First, children then understand that money does not grow on trees. Second, they learn to plan, for example saving for a bicycle. Third, they get better habits as adults and take fewer loans. Therefore both home and school should talk about money. Kind regards, [name])
- *Reusable:* Jag skriver om... / Jag tycker att..., av tre skäl. / För det första... / Därför bör...
- *Mistakes:* only two reasons; *För det första barn förstår* (verb second); no conclusion.
- *Tick-list:* ☐ topic ☐ opinion ☐ reason 1 ☐ reason 2 ☐ reason 3 ☐ conclusion.

**C8 (V theme).** *Skriv en insändare: "Dagens mode är inte snyggt." Berätta vad du tycker och ge minst tre skäl.* (Write a letter to the editor: "Today's fashion is not nice." Say what you think and give at least three reasons.)
- *Points:* topic; opinion; three reasons.
- *Model:* "Jag skriver om dagens mode. Jag tycker att det inte är särskilt snyggt, av tre skäl. För det första ser många kläder likadana ut, eftersom stora butiker säljer samma modeller. För det andra är färgerna ofta trista eller konstiga. För det tredje är kläderna dyra men går sönder snabbt. Därför önskar jag mer personliga och hållbara kläder. Med vänlig hälsning, [namn]" (I am writing about today's fashion. I think it is not particularly nice, for three reasons. First, many clothes look the same, because big shops sell the same models. Second, the colours are often dull or strange. Third, the clothes are expensive but break quickly. Therefore I wish for more personal and durable clothes. Kind regards, [name])
- *Reusable:* ...inte är särskilt... / ...eftersom... / Därför önskar jag...
- *Mistakes:* *Jag tycker att inte det är snyggt*; mixing *snygg/snyggt*.
- *Tick-list:* ☐ topic ☐ opinion ☐ three numbered reasons ☐ conclusion ☐ formal closing.

**C9 (I).** *Skriv en insändare: "Kollektivtrafiken ska vara gratis." Skriv din åsikt och minst tre skäl.* (Write a letter to the editor: "Public transport should be free." Write your opinion and at least three reasons.)
- *Model:* "Jag skriver om kollektivtrafiken. Jag tycker att den ska vara gratis i vår stad, av tre skäl. För det första skulle fler människor åka buss, och då blir det färre bilar. För det andra är det dyrt för familjer att köpa biljetter varje månad. För det tredje blir luften renare. Visst kostar det pengar, men staden sparar på vägar och miljö. Därför borde politikerna tänka om. Med vänlig hälsning, [namn]" (I am writing about public transport. I think it should be free in our city, for three reasons. First, more people would take the bus, and then there will be fewer cars. Second, it is expensive for families to buy tickets every month. Third, the air becomes cleaner. Certainly it costs money, but the city saves on roads and environment. Therefore the politicians should rethink. Kind regards, [name])
- *Reusable:* ...ska vara gratis... / Visst..., men... / Därför borde...
- *Mistakes:* *gratis* does not change with the noun; *mer billig*.
- *Tick-list:* ☐ opinion ☐ three reasons ☐ one counterpoint (optional) ☐ conclusion.

**C10 (I).** *Skriv en insändare: "Affärerna ska vara öppna på söndagar." Ge din åsikt och minst tre skäl.* (Write a letter to the editor: "Shops should be open on Sundays." Give your opinion and at least three reasons.)
- *Model:* "Jag tycker att affärerna ska vara öppna på söndagar, av tre skäl. För det första arbetar många människor på vardagarna och har bara tid att handla på helgen. För det andra blir det mindre trångt i affärerna om de är öppna längre. För det tredje får många unga ett extra jobb. Man måste förstås ge de anställda bra betalt. Därför är jag för söndagsöppet." (I think shops should be open on Sundays, for three reasons. First, many people work on weekdays and have time to shop only at the weekend. Second, it is less crowded in the shops if they are open longer. Third, many young people get an extra job. Of course, employees must be paid well. Therefore I am for Sunday opening.)
- *Reusable:* Jag tycker att... ska... / förstås / Därför är jag för...
- *Mistakes:* *affärerna ska är*; missing *av tre skäl* enumeration.
- *Tick-list:* ☐ opinion ☐ three reasons ☐ fair point ☐ conclusion.

**C11 (I).** *Skriv en insändare: "Elever ska inte ha mobiltelefon i skolan." Ge din åsikt och minst tre skäl.* (Write a letter: "Pupils should not have a mobile phone at school." Give your opinion and at least three reasons.)
- *Model:* "Jag skriver om mobiltelefoner i skolan. Jag tycker att elever inte ska ha mobilen på lektionerna, av tre skäl. För det första är det svårt att koncentrera sig när telefonen ringer eller blinkar. För det andra kan elever fuska med mobilen på prov. För det tredje pratar barn mindre med varandra på rasten. Däremot kan mobilen vara bra i nödfall. Därför tycker jag att telefonerna ska ligga i en låda under lektionen." (I am writing about mobile phones at school. Third, children talk less with each other at break. On the other hand the phone can be good in an emergency. Therefore I think the phones should be in a box during the lesson.)
- *Reusable:* ...på lektionerna / Däremot... / Därför tycker jag att...
- *Mistakes:* *i skolan/på skolan* mix; *prov* is an ett-word (*ett prov*).
- *Tick-list:* ☐ opinion ☐ three reasons ☐ contrast ☐ conclusion.

**C12 (I).** *Skriv en text om fördelar och nackdelar med att bo i en stor stad.* (Write a text about the advantages and disadvantages of living in a big city.)
- *Model:* "Att bo i en stor stad har både fördelar och nackdelar. En fördel är att det finns många arbeten, skolor och sjukhus. En annan fördel är att man kan åka kollektivt och inte behöver bil. (Living in a big city has both advantages and disadvantages. One advantage is that there are many jobs, schools and hospitals. - *Reusable:* En fördel är att... / En nackdel är att... / Jag tycker ändå att...
- *Mistakes:* only listing advantages; *fördelen* versus *fördel*.
- *Tick-list:* ☐ two advantages ☐ two disadvantages ☐ own view.

### Formal complaints, requests and messages (verified type: complaint about a bill)

**C13 (V).** *Du fick en faktura på 780 euro för en bilreparation. Skriv en reklamation. Säg vilken faktura det gäller, ge minst två skäl till att den är för hög, säg vilket belopp du tycker är rimligt och ge dina kontaktuppgifter.* (You received an invoice for 780 euros for a car repair. Write a complaint. Say which invoice it is, give at least two reasons it is too high, say what amount you think is reasonable and give your contact details.)
- *Points:* which invoice; two reasons; amount; contact details; formal opening and closing.
- *Model:* "Hej! Jag skriver om faktura 3092 från den 5 april på 780 euro. Fakturan är för hög av två skäl. För det första har ni tagit betalt för tre timmars arbete, men bilen var hos er bara en timme. För det andra stod det i offerten att bromsarna kostade 120 euro, men ni har räknat 260 euro. ## Appendix D — 10 original listening practice scenarios

**How to use.** The official listening sample audio could not be read for this report, so these are **original practice material, not official**. They imitate the three verified task types (multiple choice, true/false, short open question). A text cannot replace real audio: ask a friend to read the transcript aloud at normal speed, or use a text-to-speech app at normal speed, and listen **twice** (a practice rule; the real number of plays is not verified). Cover the transcript while you listen. Answer open questions in a few Swedish words. Read the transcript only afterwards. Then listen again to the same audio and notice the words you missed. Do not memorise the transcript: next session use new audio (Part 6A).

**D1: Voicemail from a friend.** *Transcript:* "Hej Anna, det är Rahul. Vi skulle ses klockan sex vid stationen, men jag blir sen. Bussen är försenad. Jag kommer ungefär tjugo minuter efter sex. Kan du vänta vid ingången till caféet i stället? Jag ringer igen om det blir mer än tjugo minuter. Förlåt! Vi ses snart." (Hi Anna, it is Rahul. We were going to meet at six at the station, but I will be late. The bus is delayed. I will come about twenty minutes after six. Can you wait at the café entrance instead? I will call again if it is more than twenty minutes. Sorry! See you soon.)
1. (MC) Varför är Rahul sen? A) Han har glömt tiden. B) Bussen är försenad. C) Han är sjuk. (Why is Rahul late?)
2. (Rätt/Fel) Anna ska vänta vid stationen. (Anna should wait at the station.)
3. (Open) När kommer Rahul ungefär? (When does Rahul arrive, approximately?)
*Answers:* 1 B. 2 Fel (vid ingången till caféet). 3 Ungefär tjugo minuter efter sex (about 18:20).
*Why:* the speaker says the old plan (*stationen*) and then changes it with *i stället*; listen for change-of-plan words. The time is "twenty minutes after six", not "twenty minutes".

**D2: Booking a dentist appointment.** *Transcript:* "Tandläkarmottagningen, goddag. — Hej, jag heter Karim Ali. Jag vill boka en tid. — Visst. Vi har en ledig tid på tisdag klockan nio eller på torsdag klockan halv två. — Torsdag passar bättre. Är det klockan halv två eller klockan två? — Halv två, alltså 13.30. — Bra, tack. Det blir torsdag halv två." (Dentist's surgery, good day. — Hi, my name is Karim Ali. I want to book an appointment. — Sure. We have a free time on Tuesday at nine or on Thursday at half past one. — Thursday suits better. Is it half past one or two o'clock? — Half past one, so 13:30. — Good, thanks. So it is Thursday at half past one.)
1. (MC) Vilken tid bokar kunden? A) Tisdag klockan nio. B) Torsdag klockan 13.30. C) Torsdag klockan 14.00.
2. (Rätt/Fel) Kunden väljer tisdag.
3. (Open) Vad är kundens efternamn?
*Answers:* 1 B. 2 Fel. 3 Ali.
*Why:* *halv två* is 1:30 (half *to* two), the classic trap; the receptionist repeats it as 13.30.

**D3: Station announcement.** *Transcript:* "Uppmärksamhet, tåget till Åbo, avgång klockan 14.10, är inställt. Nästa tåg till Åbo går klockan 15.10 från spår 4. Resenärer med biljett kan åka med nästa tåg. Vi ber om ursäkt för olägenheten." (Attention, the train to Turku, departure 14:10, is cancelled. The next train to Turku goes at 15:10 from track 4. Passengers with tickets may take the next train. We apologise for the inconvenience.)
1. (MC) Vad har hänt? A) Tåget är tio minuter försenat. B) Tåget är inställt. C) Spåret har ändrats.
2. (Rätt/Fel) Nästa tåg går klockan 14.10.
3. (Open) Från vilket spår går nästa tåg?
*Answers:* 1 B. 2 Fel (15.10). 3 Spår 4.
*Why:* two times are mentioned; the first is the cancelled train. *Inställt* = cancelled.

**D4: Radio advertisement.** *Transcript:* "Stora rean på Möbelhuset börjar på fredag! Alla soffor kostar 30 procent mindre. En vanlig soffa kostar 500 euro, men under rean kostar den bara 350 euro. Affären är öppen från klockan 10 till 20 på fredag och lördag, men stängd på söndag. Kom gärna tidigt!" (The big sale at the Furniture House starts on Friday! All sofas cost 30 percent less. A normal sofa costs 500 euros, but during the sale it costs only 350 euros. The shop is open from 10 to 20 on Friday and Saturday, but closed on Sunday. Come early!)
1. (MC) Hur mycket kostar en soffa under rean? A) 500 euro. B) 350 euro. C) 150 euro.
2. (Rätt/Fel) Affären är öppen på söndag.
3. (Open) Hur många procent billigare är sofforna?
*Answers:* 1 B. 2 Fel. 3 30 procent.
*Why:* three numbers (500, 350, 30%) compete; the question asks which one; "stängd" negates Sunday.

**D5: A neighbour talks.** *Transcript:* "Hej, jag bor i lägenheten under dig. Jag har ett litet problem. Det är mycket ljud från din lägenhet efter klockan tio på kvällarna. Jag måste gå upp tidigt och kan inte sova. — Åh, förlåt, det visste jag inte. Barnen leker ofta sent. Jag ska prata med dem. — Tack, det vore bra. — Jag lovar att det blir tystare från och med i kväll." (Hi, I live in the flat below you. I have a small problem.
