# Full textbook reading map

This is a page-by-page reading record for physical pages 4-55 of
`docs/text-book-images/text-book.pdf`. Pages 1-3 are cover/front matter and page
56 is the back cover. The matching visual references are
`docs/text-book-images/page-004.png` through `page-055.png`.

The book was read for future planning only. This map does **not** activate a
lecture, assign homework, or authorize copying its dialogue into Stigen.

of these four. Service workers, relatives, classmates, and other situational
speakers remain unnamed roles rather than permanent characters.

## Whole-book cast conclusion

The language functions on all 52 content pages can be taught with Alex, Elin,
Henrik, and Maja plus unnamed role labels. The source's large cast is necessary
only if its exact family/affair plot is reproduced, which Stigen should not do.

Maja fills the one recurring gap found by the complete review: school, exams,
parties, friends, hobbies, household rules, future study, and environment. No
fifth permanent character is justified by the current textbook.

Source continuity is not always explicit enough to carry over safely. For
example, page 39 says Peter bought a laptop for Jonas even though pages 28 and
50 establish Andreas and Lena as Jonas's parents. Page 22 leaves Nora's date
unnamed. These ambiguities reinforce the decision to map teaching functions,
not copy identities or infer relationships.
