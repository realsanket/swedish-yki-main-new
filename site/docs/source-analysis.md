# Source relationship and curriculum decisions

- `docs/Group 3.md` is the canonical cumulative classroom notebook. It contains all 51 dated lessons, all ten native Word tables, and a Markdown transcription of the writing-style chart that existed only as an embedded image. A full audit against the former Word and PDF versions found every substantial Word paragraph present and 99.95% normalized PDF-text coverage; the unmatched PDF tokens were page or link artifacts. Trace references by lesson heading rather than legacy PDF page number. The retired originals remain recoverable in `backup/source-originals-2026-09-29/`.
- `docs/text-book-images/text-book.pdf` is the beginner foundation. The classroom notebook follows it most closely in Lectures 1–22 (roughly notebook pages 2–64), covering early A1/A2 Swedish.
- `e-kirja-Forbered-dig-for-allman-sprakexamen-QR-16.02.2025-yyyiif (1).pdf` is the later YKI-oriented source. Lecture 23 bridges into test structure and register; Lectures 24–45 follow its speaking and writing themes (roughly notebook pages 69–132).
- Lectures 46–51 in the notebook are labelled older YKI tasks. They are classroom material, not verified current official specifications.

## Important caveats

The classroom notebook is not a self-contained course. It refers to teaching, recordings, grammar handouts, Quizlet, Google Classroom, and homework that are not fully present in the files. Exact classroom timing and exam claims therefore cannot be treated as verified requirements.

The YKI preparation book concentrates on speaking and writing. Stigen supplies newly authored listening and reading work so all four skills develop throughout the course.

The YKI book’s contents page appears to place two later sections in the wrong order. The physical sequence is work and education first, then leisure; the classroom notebook follows that physical order.

## What Stigen transfers

Stigen keeps the strongest structural ideas:

1. cumulative sequencing from beginner Swedish into practical YKI-oriented themes;
2. small explanations followed by worked contrasts, guided production, independent use, and homework;
3. recurring everyday domains such as home, work, study, services, health, travel, leisure, and society;
4. explicit practice in register, audience, purpose, reasons, examples, and repair strategies.

## What Stigen does not copy

The source books are copyrighted references. Stigen does not republish their pages, exercises, answer keys, QR media, or long passages. All learner-facing scenes, questions, listening scripts, writing prompts, speaking prompts, checkpoints, and mock simulations are original.

Current test facts come from the Finnish National Agency for Education, not from the classroom notes. Stigen’s YKI-style work is practice evidence only: it is not an official examination, grade, readiness prediction, or CEFR certificate.
