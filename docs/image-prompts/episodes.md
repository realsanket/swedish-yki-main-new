# Lecture pictures (Lectures 3-7)

Lectures 1 and 2 already have pictures. Save each new picture in
`site/public/images/story/episodes/` with the file name shown. Start every
prompt with the style block and cast block from [`README.md`](README.md). Each
lecture's object label (`story.object`) is already set in its JSON; only
`story.art` waits for the file.

## Lecture 3: The evening before the test

**File:** `episode-03-evening-before-test.webp` · **Object:** study notes and a phone

> A spring evening in a cosy Helsinki flat, soft fading daylight through a large
> window. Alex sits at a light-wood desk, tired but smiling, rubbing one eye and
> holding a pen over his open spiral notebook; an open textbook, blank study
> cards and a glass of water beside him. An open laptop shows a video call with
> Elin, who smiles kindly and rests her cheek on her pressed-together hands in a
> friendly "time to sleep" gesture. No text on the book, cards or screen.

## Lecture 4: Coffee after class

**File:** `episode-04-coffee-after-class.webp` · **Object:** weekend plans over coffee

> A small Nordic café after an evening class, light-wood table, plants on the
> windowsill, a jar of cinnamon buns on the counter behind. Alex leans forward
> with a relaxed smile; his spiral notebook lies open beside a closed laptop,
> and a game controller peeks out of his bag. Elin holds a paperback and
> gestures cheerfully toward the window, as if suggesting the cinema. Two white
> coffee cups. Through the window, a soft blurred evening street with warm
> lights. No signage or text.

## Lecture 5: The old camera

**File:** `episode-05-old-camera.webp` · **Object:** an old camera

> A cosy Nordic café corner after class, three coffee cups on a light-wood
> table. Alex proudly holds out a small, scuffed vintage camera and shows a
> printed photo of the Helsinki waterfront. Maja, meeting him for the first
> time, leans in delighted and points at the photo, her mustard backpack on the
> chair. Elin sits beside them, smiling as she introduces them.

## Lecture 6: Good news

**File:** `episode-06-good-news.webp` · **Object:** a passed maths test

> A sunny Nordic café corner in the morning. Maja beams and holds up a marked
> school paper that shows only a large red tick (no letters or numbers). Elin
> claps her hands in delight. Alex smiles and raises his coffee cup in a toast,
> his spiral notebook open on the table. Three coffee cups. Happy, celebratory
> mood.

## Lecture 7: The dinner invitation

**File:** `episode-07-dinner-invitation.webp` · **Object:** flowers and a bottle of wine

> A cosy Nordic kitchen, soft daylight through a tall window, light-wood table
> and shelves. Alex has just arrived and holds out five tulips and a paper bag
> with six red apples; a bottle of red wine and a bottle of apple juice stand on
> the table with a stack of plates and four glasses. Elin smiles warmly as she
> takes the flowers, a mixing bowl and an apple-cake tin beside her. Maja waves
> happily from the doorway.
