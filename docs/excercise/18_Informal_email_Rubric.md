# Informal email rubric — 10 points

## Informal text structure — 4 points

Skills: Greeting + taking initiative + vocabulary + reactions or emotions

- **4 pts:** has all 4 skills
- **3 pts:** Reacting, showing emotion; has 3 out of 4 skills
- **2 pts:** Being specific; has 2 out of 4 skills
- **1 pt:** Asking informal questions, giving suggestions to meet; has 1 out of 4 skills

## Knowing the difference between formal and informal — 3 points

Ensure level 3 (B1).

- **3 pts — Mastered:** Add background information + subjunctions
- **2 pts — Doing well:** Sentence structure is complex enough / enough background
- **1 pt — Needs work:** A2

## Grammar — 3 points

- **3 pts:** (Near) perfect
- **2 pts:** Occassional issues in word choice or prepositions
- **1 pt:** Spelling errors and systematic errors, wrong tense usage
