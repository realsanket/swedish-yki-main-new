## Instructions

Diary
No due date
Posted Apr 27
Write about your daily life. The goal is to use the past tense (especially irregular verbs) and to obtain vocabulary you would need to talk about yourself, your habits, likes and dislikes, as well as about people in your life. These are small steps you can take to ensure that you will be able to speak and write confidently on the exam, as it aids you in transferring your most common thoughts to Swedish.
