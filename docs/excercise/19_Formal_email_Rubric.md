# Formal email rubric — 10 points

## Formal text structure — 4 points

Skills: Greeting + Presentation + demands/questions + vocabulary/facts/numbers

- **4 pts:** 4 out of 4 skills; add facts, numbers
- **3 pts:** 3 out of 4 skills; Being specific
- **2 pts:** 2 out of 4 skills; Present relevant context
- **1 pt:** 1 out of 4 skills

## Knowing the difference between formal and informal — 3 points

Ensure level 3 (B1).

- **3 pts — Mastered:** Add background information + subjunctions
- **2 pts — Doing well:** Sentence structure is complex enough or good background
- **1 pt — Needs work:** A2

## Grammar — 3 points

- **3 pts:** (Near) perfect
- **2 pts:** Occassional issues in word choice or prepositions
- **1 pt:** Spelling errors and systematic errors, wrong tense usage
