# Swedish YKI exam-first course — critical audit and improvement specification

**Audit date:** 15 November 2026. **Objective:** obtain Swedish YKI intermediate grade 3 in an accepted citizenship pair, with minimum wasted preparation. This is an audit and proposed redesign, not an official YKI curriculum or a guarantee of passing.

## Scope and evidence

Four supplied files (approximately 94,386 whitespace-delimited words) were loaded and structurally checked. The audit examines their schedule, task banks, gates, grammar explanations, selected Swedish models, book assignments and cross-references. The actual Gimara textbook, its audio, the learner's performance, teacher feedback and the referenced `assignments.json` were not supplied. I cannot verify the original book's completeness, every Swedish phrase's naturalness or every unseen exercise's validity. A qualified Swedish teacher should review the final learner-facing sentences. The audit identifies concrete errors, internal contradictions, design risks and improvements separately; it is not an exhaustive native-speaker copyedit of 94,000 words.

Source abbreviations: **P** = YKI-EXAM-FIRST-PLAN.md; **C** = YKI-EXAM-FIRST-CURRICULUM.md; **B** = YKI-BOOK-MAP.md; **T** = YKI-NEW-TEMPLATES-T17-T20.md. Line numbers refer to the uploaded originals (not this audit). Official sources are at the end.

## 1. Executive decisions

1. Retain the underlying function-based approach, substitution ladder, typed practice, exact task interpretation, independent feedback and grade-pair strategy.
2. Replace the 84-day fixed content schedule with a competency-based core. A day is a practice unit, not a promise of mastery. Make a true A0 entry branch and an accelerated A1/A2 branch.
3. Reduce 20 IDs to 6 productive answer engines with reusable micro-moves. Keep all 20 as references, not 20 separate scripts to recite.
4. Move native audio, comprehension of question intent, personal high-frequency vocabulary and listening/responding to unexpected turns to the start.
5. Practise 2–3 target skills adaptively, not all four equally. Speaking/writing is an initial hypothesis, not universally the easiest pair. Evaluate reading and listening seriously enough to detect a better accepted pair.
6. Run short unseen transfer checks from week 1 and obtain independent assessment before registration. Keep mock prompts and scoring keys outside the learner material. Treat 84 days as a core cycle, not an exam-ready deadline.
7. Use the practice book as an optional task reservoir. Never aim for a book completion rate at the expense of mastery.

### Cross-cutting corrections

- Do not call distinct required word-count targets official. Some curriculum days make 100–150 compulsory even though the plan says target only. Match actual instructions and coherent development.
- Do not demand a rescue phrase if the speaker has no problem (Day 12). A natural answer without rescue is better.
- Avoid forcing a literal connector (*eftersom* with *inte/redan* in every clause, Day 24 C 823); e.g. *eftersom jag jobbar* is perfectly correct.
- Many written question drills show model answers next to prompts; separate learning mode from testing mode. An independent task must not expose English translation if the goal is Swedish comprehension.
- Keep enough recurring audio input, varied speakers, and phonological feedback from Day 1. No requirement for a native accent; clear sound/word stress/length matter.
- Use fictional identity/contact details for mock calls; no need to disclose a real address or phone number unless actually needed outside training.

## 7. Swedish language corrections and precision

These are targeted examples, not a completed native-speaker review of all examples. Do not drill the 'before' column as correct Swedish.

| Location | Issue | Better teaching explanation / replacement |
|---|---|---|
| P G1, G10 | V2 described as 'verb in second place' may be understood as second written word. | Finite verb is second **constituent** in a declarative main clause: *På fredag efter jobbet går jag hem.* Not a second-word rule; questions and subordinate clauses differ. |
| P 1282 | Reason given for *Jag är bor i Esbo* = 'One verb only'. | Incorrect explanation: a clause can have several verbs. Swedish present-tense lexical verb needs no auxiliary *är*: *Jag bor i Esbo*; *Jag vill bo i Esbo* is valid. |
| P G3; C 38,68 | 'Question word then verb' is simplified; subject questions and multiword question phrases need care. | *Vem ringde? Vad hände? Hur många personer kommer?* In indirect subject questions: *Jag vet inte vem som ringde / vad som hände*. |
| P G2; C 76 | All adverbials treated as identical to *inte*. | Teach normal position for short common clauses, not invariant syntax. Contrast *Jag har redan betalat* / *eftersom jag redan har betalat*. |
| P G6 | Heading 'perfect with no time' is overgeneralized. | Present perfect may include ongoing time expressions (*i dag, den här veckan, sedan i måndags*). Past for a completed time: *Jag ringde i går*. |
| P G9; T 110 | *på lördag* = this Saturday always; *på lördagar* = every Saturday. | Common contrast upcoming Saturday vs Saturdays in general; context can change the exact date. |
| P T3/T4/T5; T18 121–131 and T20b | Noun substitutions always use singular common-gender pronoun/adjective. | Make noun cards with agreement: *en cykel – den är trasig; ett kylskåp – det är trasigt; två skor – de är trasiga*. Repeat noun if uncertain. |
| P T7 | *Tack, gärna! Jag kommer gärna.* repetitive. | *Tack, jag kommer gärna!* or *Tack, det passar bra.* |
| P T12 | *Jag vill anmäla att min plånbok är borta* at lost property is unnecessarily bureaucratic. | *Jag har tappat bort min plånbok. Har ni hittat den?* Use *anmäla* for formal report where appropriate. |
| P/T formal address | *Bästa mottagare* grammatical but generic and stilted. | For ordinary customer emails, simple *Hej!* and *Med vänliga hälsningar* are sufficient. For an official appeal, use addressee when known. |
| T 59 | Calls a reserved seat ticket *platsbiljett*. | Usable, but *Jag har bokat den här platsen. Här är min biljett.* easier. |
| T 66 | Thanks 'det var snällt av dig' before knowing neighbour complied; also wet washing removed, waiting may not undo harm. | *Skulle du kunna låta mig tvätta klart? Jag har bokat tiden till nio. Tack.* Adapt request to actual situation. |
| T 103 | *min vanliga vardag* redundant. | *min vardag* or *en vanlig dag i mitt liv*. |
| T 123–131 | *Den ligger... Den är...* not transferable unchanged to *mitt kvarter* (ett). | Mark pronoun/adjective dependency; *Mitt kvarter ligger nära centrum. Det är lugnt.* |
| T 228 | *så jag vill säga upp mig* then last day can sound tentative. | If task is a definite resignation: *Jag har fått ett nytt jobb och ska därför sluta här.* |
| T 242 | *Kan du berätta vad du gör innan du börjar?* understandable but less explicit for advance explanation. | *Kan du förklara vad du ska göra innan du börjar?* |
| T 250,268 | Comma before *av tre skäl* not needed. | *Jag tycker att beslutet är fel av tre skäl.* Softer formal alternative: *Jag ber er att ompröva beslutet av följande skäl.* |
| T 258 | *du har den sista stolen av den här modellen* is possible, but not always physically in hand. | Match situation: *Jag ser att du tänker köpa den sista stolen.* |
| C 1869 | *ett lån* 'a loan' in library task may suggest financial loan. | Teach *låna en bok*, *förlänga lånet*, *lämna tillbaka boken* together. |
| C 823 | Requires *inte* or *redan* before verb in every *eftersom* clause. | These words are optional; teach position only if used. *Jag kan inte komma eftersom jag jobbar.* |
| Plan models, all | Personal origin, profession, family examples can be mistaken for learner facts. | Explicitly use placeholders and personalized truthful details in personal tasks; hypothetical details are appropriate where the task gives an imagined scenario. |

### Missing high-value language modules

These are productive gaps rather than blanket grammar deficiencies. Teach through ready chunks, not full tables:

- Pronouns and object forms: *jag/mig, du/dig, han/honom, hon/henne, vi/oss, de/dem*; common *sin/sitt/sina* vs *hans/hennes* only as needed.
- Gender/number and references: *den/det/de*, *den här/det här/de här*, *en/ett* with whole noun phrases. Include *den/det* after previously introduced objects, plural agreement.
- Modal and action verbs as productive chunks, add missing *behöver, försöker, förstår, lär mig, hittar, frågar, förklarar, berättar, läser, lyssnar, säger, tycker om*; preserve corresponding common forms.
- Prepositions in context: *på jobbet, i Finland, till jobbet, från jobbet, hos läkaren, med bussen, rädd för, intresserad av, nöjd med*.
- Negation/quantifiers: *ingen/inget/inga, någon/något/några, mycket/många, lite/få*, *inte längre, ännu inte, inte alls*.
- Comparison, time and direction: *bättre/sämre, billigare/dyrare, lika ... som, mer/mindre än, innan/efter, först/sedan, var/vart*.
- Question decoder: command → expected output; audience and role; asked details; negative/polarity and direct/indirect question; *svara jakande/nekande, säg till, be om, motivera*.
- Pronunciation and spoken recognition: stressed syllables, vowel length, å/ä/ö, difficult minimal pairs, natural phrase elision and Finland-Swedish variants; use qualified audio and comprehensibility feedback rather than English phonetic spellings.

## 9. New learning mechanics: techniques that can save time legitimately

1. **One meaning, one reliable production form.** Learn *Jag tycker att...* before six formal synonyms. Recognize synonyms in questions rather than needing to produce them all.
2. **Audio → intention → reply.** Before typing practice, listen to a short prompt without text, identify what it asks, then respond. Next time switch time/role/polarity. Check transcription only after training detection.
3. **Transform instead of translate.** *Jag kan komma på tisdag* → *Jag kan inte komma på tisdag* → *Kan jag komma på onsdag i stället?* → *På onsdag kan jag komma efter fyra.* This is more reusable than separate isolated sentences.
4. **Prompt decoder: WHO, ROLE, ACTION, DETAILS, TONE.** Underline required points, select a response engine and answer the requested action first. No unrequested generic paragraph.
5. **Answer in layers.** Minimum relevant reply first, then reason, then concrete detail. Short task: one or two layers; long task: develop a connected narrative/opinion. Avoid word salad and reciting fillers.
6. **Paired contrast.** Practise caller vs receiver, buyer vs seller, accept vs refuse, before vs after, works vs broken. An examiner's question change should change your answer.
7. **One fact in three tenses.** *Jag jobbar hemma. I går jobbade jag hemma. Jag har jobbat hemma i två år.* Learn in context rather than speed-recite 120 forms.
8. **Personal narrative seeds.** Prepare real facts about work, housing, family/friends, routines, good and bad experiences. Use them across questions only when relevant. Never treat a memorised paragraph as a fixed answer.
9. **Spaced recall with transfer.** Day +1/+3/+7/+14/+30 approximate; use a new task to check delayed retention. An exact copied sentence is not mastery.
10. **Mistake triage.** First wrong meaning, misunderstanding/omission, intelligibility; then high-frequency grammar; then low-impact stylistic details. Fix repeated patterns, not every typo with a new card.
11. **Digital mode practice.** Swedish keyboard layout, å ä ö, select/edit/delete, no predictive assistance; type under time without tools. Use short proofreading in actual prompt context.
12. **Reserve testing material.** Keep an independent bank of unseen Swedish-only cards and audio; never put solutions in learner sheet. Rotate speakers, topics and instruction wording.

## 10. Proposed 84-day adaptive cycle (not a promise)

| Segment | Focus | Observable exit |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 | 10–20 min screener; determine ability | Can recognize common questions, say/ type simple sentences? If yes, proceed to a broader four-skill diagnostic. |
| Days 1–14 | 24–36 starter chunks, sounds/audio, basic questions, present/modals/negation, early mini tasks | Independently answer basic questions; 3–6 connected relevant sentences; recognize basic spoken commands. A0 can repeat without penalty. |
| Days 15–28 | Request, transaction, complaint, apology/invitation; common past/future; personal story | Independently deliver a short reply and simple message; handle one unexpected turn. Mini mock 1 with fresh cards. |
| Days 29–42 | Opinion, description/routine, story; simple reasons and examples; formal vs friend tone; comparison | Understand Swedish-only prompts; complete relevant answers of different lengths with teacher feedback. |
| Days 43–56 | Topic transfer across all nine official domains; expand weak vocabulary, varied audio and reading | New context and role changes without script dependence. Comparative diagnostic of all four skills. |
| Days 57–70 | Focus on weakest skill within best valid pair; part-mocks weekly; fix key mistakes | Teacher-referenced estimate approaches B1 across several new tasks. |
| Days 71–84 | Independent parallel mocks, digital interface, stamina, booking decision | Teacher reports consistent B1-level communication in one accepted pair; no serious recurrent task gap. If not ready continue targeted remediation in 7–14 day blocks. |

### Daily 60-minute schedule

- 10 min spaced recall and native audio shadowing (short phrases already learned).
- 10 min new active patterns: 3–6 chunks plus one variation.
- 10 min Swedish question/recorded dialogue comprehension, response without translation.
- 15 min speaking task, review and one redo.
- 12 min typed task, feedback/check and edit.
- 3 min log: top one mistake to correct tomorrow and 1–3 cards.

For a 90-minute day add 15 min on the weakest target skill and 15 min on a longer new task or teacher feedback. For 120 min add a further 15 min on a second varied audio/input task and 15 min on independent productive output. Use practice book to REPLACE matching tasks. A proper full practice mock may require 2–2.5 hours or split sessions. A 60-minute lesson is not a mock equivalent.

### Simplified Day 1 for a true A0 learner

Goal: understand four personal questions and independently give four short answers. Learn *Jag heter ...; Jag bor i ...; Jag kommer från ...; Jag talar ...; Jag lär mig svenska.* Model correct pronunciation with native audio. Listen for *Vad heter du? Var bor du? Varifrån kommer du? Vilka språk talar du?* Answer in a full sentence in training. Swap names, places and languages; speak without looking; type three lines without help. A mini task on Swedish-only card: *Presentera dig. Berätta vad du heter, var du bor och vilka språk du talar.* Count task points and listener intelligibility. If the learner cannot do this independently, repeat tomorrow with new details. No complex V2, perfect, 21 numbers or 60-second monologue on Day 1 unless learner demonstrates prior ability.

### Readiness not completion

Use a separate teacher rating sheet covering task fit, connected ideas, functional range, comprehensibility, fluency/pronunciation (speaking), grammar and lexical precision, and overall estimated level. Measure from several unseen tasks across contexts, not a single flawless answer. Ask the teacher specifically whether errors prevent comprehension, whether language works outside routine cases, and whether the speaker keeps going independently. For reading/listening, a practice score is diagnostic, not an official YKI cut score. Any readiness estimate is provisional.
